Every user would be able to access the documents to [delete](#h_5ed67c9fd0) or [recover](#h_382ae443ed) documents.

Click the menu icon **≡** on the top left corner to unfold the Menu, get entry to the **Documents** page.

![Delete or recover Documents](/images/kb/103000035040/B7xm1VU-q9_0pbsB5rhr2KSV6x-47rGdeA.jpeg)

## Delete

Right-click on the folder/document you wish to delete, click **DELETE**.

![Delete or recover Documents](/images/kb/103000035040/r63wf_q-jENFJUSGsSqJnXkmQMXKtcPFrg.jpeg)

![Delete or recover Documents](/images/kb/103000035040/W6euIkw87dSR9om6_0Mj2MdSpD24_a_d8g.jpeg)

**DELETE** to confirm.

![Delete or recover Documents](/images/kb/103000035040/cvZwjdKNkd-tXfRkVT5armGB4Q-Uusj71A.jpeg)

or you may also left-click to open the document, click the ? icon to delete.

![Delete or recover Documents](/images/kb/103000035040/CkNWGOWSnskDGKlOxdnO6c1jneNkxjba7w.jpeg)

![Delete or recover Documents](/images/kb/103000035040/tLWK2l6Z7pN4szO3U9GHzftdo3YRDD5s6Q.jpeg)

**DELETE** to confirm.

![Delete or recover Documents](/images/kb/103000035040/uyzSn41iYI-gGXHfARJ4h9fwk7hN27fTUA.jpeg)

* * *

## Recover from trash

Click **GO TO TRASH** to access trash.

![Delete or recover Documents](/images/kb/103000035040/CHbsBDpDDxyjD8cwiPTdPnJtNA0MhSTK0Q.jpeg)

Select the folder/document you want to recover, and click **RECOVER.**

![Delete or recover Documents](/images/kb/103000035040/L4MekA6m6Px5GDl8dDweT62Ypkg0r7nSxg.jpeg)

The selected will be recovered to the original place.
