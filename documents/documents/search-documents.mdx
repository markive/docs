The uploaded documents can be searched on the system.  
First of all, may I please know which method are you using RDrive?

[via Website](#h_be6e314fa8)

[via APP](#h_81967e3d99)

* * *

## via **Website**

Click the menu icon **≡** on the top left corner to unfold the Menu, and get entry to the **Documents** page.

![Search documents](/images/kb/103000035033/btnRXDf4pHP8gziRgjuVl2selnO_8QINzA.jpeg)

![Search documents](/images/kb/103000035033/zHnSivfKf8IpKvVSdpSW_ZDVm-TEfufi_Q.jpeg)

You may type the keyword of the document you would like to search at the **unified search bar** at the top.

![Search documents](/images/kb/103000035033/f1TM7x1ch94j0Vt5bh4sRneP1denUc-toQ.jpeg)

Search results will be shown below.

![Search documents](/images/kb/103000035033/o7oAeRlheNtQfNEX29MZdh8Ee8zh8CcXFQ.jpeg)

* * *

## via APP

  
Choose **Document Repository**.

![Search documents](/images/kb/103000035033/3FH37m1LXdoNIJafhSetXO5HgibEK8oYCg.jpeg)

Click on the **Search** icon, the fourth tab.

You may type the keyword of the document you would like to search at the **search bar** at the top.

![Search documents](/images/kb/103000035033/yYRlcf1ly6SLIwHehrnDNi9aPeU9qh2NbA.jpeg)

Search results will be shown below.

![Search documents](/images/kb/103000035033/hQTGiNBzZ8R1RRcfTi1m8v0vSB9v_eSPMw.jpeg)
