## via APP

By default, your device will **not** download documents that are uploaded to the document repository. If you want to access them offline, be sure to download them beforehand.

  
Click the **Document repository** button.

![How to download documents?](/images/kb/103000035037/j_eGSnafL9KJyDvNFXH0RVCAr5xSdPRysQ.jpeg)

Select document and tap on it to download.

![How to download documents?](/images/kb/103000035037/2dBzpQjjyFO_OiWTAucbUV_Swpqj6KuCYA.jpeg)

![How to download documents?](/images/kb/103000035037/WzfM3Xbm3KVFUD8VpPo5-OLfqHdDVlMz5A.jpeg)

When you tap to open a file for the first time, you will be downloading it given there is an internet connection.

Once downloaded they will be stored on the device.

![How to download documents?](/images/kb/103000035037/NndEJ3x1ka0PvLC8vecEeTq3VsrxuWPfkQ.jpeg)

![How to download documents?](/images/kb/103000035037/GcQQPKnZbU0vA2XdGwChV11yAfBVwOH0-Q.jpeg)

The downloaded documents will also show on the third tab, **Downloads**.

![How to download documents?](/images/kb/103000035037/yS-2b0vbbiiAqGaa97nI7HEEs3WRbDPzXQ.jpeg)

 _✨Tips: If the files are into bundles, you can download multiple files in one go._ 

_[Simply go to Bundles and click **Download All**.](https://docs.rdrive.io/documents/bundles/download-a-bundle)_
