---
title: "How to upload documents?"
description: "Go to Documents, tap on the folder you wish to upload your file to, and click UPLOAD."
---

Go to **Documents**, tap on the [folder](https://snagr-support.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions/articles/103000035036-how-to-create-a-folder-) you wish to upload your file to, and click **UPLOAD**.

![How to upload documents?](/images/kb/103000035035/s73QftAcIXOWHBKRfD75GahWrhhx3HLOjw.jpeg)

![How to upload documents?](/images/kb/103000035035/kNQMLH2iLY2Bet7F1NXCTmSpyZmtAV6BFw.jpeg)

The upload dialogue pops up for you to upload the document(s).

![How to upload documents?](/images/kb/103000035035/zjBNYOatC5JY2oTq0PxOkFJcJpFCbxxbxQ.jpeg)

You may also upload the document(s) by dragging and dropping it from your computer.

![How to upload documents?](/images/kb/103000035035/hRAIk3zOReDtzfAbAgf_nTvx7sGL_9R2pg.jpeg)
