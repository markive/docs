You may [edit the name](#h_367c0dcf20) and [dot colour](#h_e9df2461c0) of the bundle or [delete](#h_38f08a5694) it.

First of all, access the Documents page by clicking the menu icon **≡** on the top left corner ➡ **Documents**.

![Edit the bundle](/images/kb/103000035030/UGJES9J4QYHWygw4RQ6Or70YJt1OpS4OZw.jpeg)

Unfold the bundle window.

![Edit the bundle](/images/kb/103000035030/yiQCzK-RR16dLzqcUmrlddSnDxYNF3l2Xg.jpeg)

## Edit the name

Click the **⋮** button and select **Edit bundle name**.

![Edit the bundle](/images/kb/103000035030/xj1ov0yHRSuU6rV-LM4Q3DQhY0hrMvxKNQ.jpeg)

![Edit the bundle](/images/kb/103000035030/BGP76ZVBXCaRSFaiorewmFK98tDLXEYl7A.jpeg)

Edit the name and click **EDIT** to save.

![Edit the bundle](/images/kb/103000035030/QJGkEq4sPJmD-6Sys1fgej_QZLtfhHjvew.jpeg)

* * *

## Change the dot colour

Click on the current dot colour.

![Edit the bundle](/images/kb/103000035030/V0WAYZzBfFg4LH4cfB4Z4b3VBOnh9qbXWg.jpeg)

the colour selection will pop up for you to select.

You may click on it to change.

![Edit the bundle](/images/kb/103000035030/Li6tMYAwdohREOS8JCxxauq5BikTNhtAbw.jpeg)

* * *

## Delete

Click the **⋮** button and select Delete.

![Edit the bundle](/images/kb/103000035030/eFHUkUm5W2L-HrUPSQiFz88A8yWBCLP4EA.jpeg)

![Edit the bundle](/images/kb/103000035030/KZlBwyC_dz6iMODjZI8V-fu-0aLPd41xLQ.jpeg)

**DELETE** to delete.

![Edit the bundle](/images/kb/103000035030/ZExEmsDjJgpYUYvf5ePC8Rn0pzg_yLkVCg.jpeg)
