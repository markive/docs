You may add the uploaded documents to the bundle or [remove](#h_ffcc8384b8) from the bundle.

First of all, access the Documents page by clicking the menu icon **≡** on the top left corner ➡ **Documents**.

![Manager documents in the Bundle.](/images/kb/103000035031/OoOOK7_VPEtivirhG5Kglo6tEsPMYWvdqQ.jpeg)

## ADD

To add documents to the bundle, you would be able to do [per folder](#h_3963662b4e) and documents.

-   Folder

Right-click on the folder you would like to add to the bundle.

![Manager documents in the Bundle.](/images/kb/103000035031/hNareh0W4n1PuV_Mg1btQU9EOje5euVk2A.jpeg)

Click **Add to bundle.**

![Manager documents in the Bundle.](/images/kb/103000035031/Yu225eH5DDrXCZCBPbtob946ILddteYY4A.jpeg)

Select the bundle to add to.

**OK** to confirm.

-   Per documents

To add documents to the bundle, you may add by a [click](#h_2517d45a36) or [drag and drop.](#h_ad0b2f7c38)

## 。 Click

To add by click, please select the documents you need to add and click **Add to bundle.**

![Manager documents in the Bundle.](/images/kb/103000035031/9oURX-owGHUdGOUhwQxiVMVfAyQW6EN4kA.jpeg)

Select the bundle to add to.

**OK** to confirm.

![Manager documents in the Bundle.](/images/kb/103000035031/CoipohXtDTep28kgRGej2gDFBjBRm19Lsw.jpeg)

## 。 Drag and drop

or select the documents you need to add, unfold the bundle window, and drag the file over.

![Manager documents in the Bundle.](/images/kb/103000035031/ibI5Lsw96wCD9ewDVfWVDFAhkx6h-DAECg.gif)

Done.

The added items will have a dot as same as the bundle dot colour.

![Manager documents in the Bundle.](/images/kb/103000035031/Is747QLuDHIUF86ZVZpm6yv86OB1Vnjabw.jpeg)

* * *

## REMOVE

To remove the document from the bundle, just need to unfold it and click the ? to remove.
