Click the menu icon **≡** on the top left corner to unfold the Menu, get entry to the **Documents** page to create a Bundle.

Unfold the bundle window.

![Create a Bundle](/images/kb/103000035032/Jn65WMuR-9Bq5RUXrBwjEUBVnHaJ9aMufQ.jpeg)

Click the ✚ button

![Create a Bundle](/images/kb/103000035032/OIGMPXBATr677XI3yaMXgzgV0QvDVwomsQ.jpeg)

Enter the name, **ADD** to create.

![Create a Bundle](/images/kb/103000035032/gxwHsdygDyGRaai5yTcW7dqdlXmiN-9bpw.jpeg)

DONE.

![Create a Bundle](/images/kb/103000035032/DLwnfLh6gIUYdFfLTK04fVLtwxPGVBlrag.jpeg)

After the bundle is created, you may [edit the name and dot colour.](https://docs.rdrive.io/documents/bundles/manager-documents-in-the-bundle)

[And you would be able to add documents to it.](https://docs.rdrive.io/documents/bundles/manager-documents-in-the-bundle)
