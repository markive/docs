If the files are into **[bundles](https://docs.rdrive.io/documents/bundles/manager-documents-in-the-bundle)**, you can download multiple files in one go.

  
First of all, may I please know which method are you using RDrive?

-   [via Website](#h_124d8bed08)
    
-   [via APP](#h_d416c49a07)
    

* * *

## via **Website**

Click the menu icon **≡** on the top left corner to unfold the Menu, get entry to the **Documents** page.

![Download a bundle](/images/kb/103000035029/xWSlxE6T45OQsZJMzrpCuIUExUSFA3eWAQ.jpeg)

![Download a bundle](/images/kb/103000035029/8aLkolHd1fv1w380duBbTINum36zdDIfpw.jpeg)

Unfold the bundle window

![Download a bundle](/images/kb/103000035029/bOdJIy83GjvmvFDv7EwiStYnETOX0fIchw.jpeg)

Click the **⋮** button and select **Download** to start downloading.

![Download a bundle](/images/kb/103000035029/H0zq_tdyHJm_rQ3FP41PlitGr6l5JFZxGA.jpeg)

![Download a bundle](/images/kb/103000035029/rQsltuTTcacP_veybJqreyTc4sfR_TW-Sg.jpeg)

* * *

## via APP

By default, your device will **not** download documents that are uploaded to the document repository. If you want to access them offline, be sure to download them beforehand.

Click on the **Document Repository** button.

![Download a bundle](/images/kb/103000035029/Mmg69gwiniGQ5dMPsyZBEw4c4iPaOshLeg.jpeg)

Click on the second tab, **Bundle.**

Select the bundle you want to download.

![Download a bundle](/images/kb/103000035029/nslha4pC3rzPuev0tuI7an9tOu3vxAkQTQ.jpeg)

Tap the Download all button.

![Download a bundle](/images/kb/103000035029/RMDTcgIDv-El_cWAuQLjwkub-hoPWOAgKw.jpeg)

**OK** to confirm the download.

Once downloaded they will be stored on the device.

![Download a bundle](/images/kb/103000035029/IQo_pd42QSqBLAKYLpjzazvVyT8bEglI8w.jpeg)

The downloaded documents will also show on the third tab, **Downloads**.

![Download a bundle](/images/kb/103000035029/6355KnPTwcT9apXnbPP29jv0SUC0H0dZzg.jpeg)
