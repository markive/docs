**Overview of the Company-Wide Process Analysis Dashboard**

  

This dashboard delivers a structured and insightful overview of process management across projects, focusing on form statuses, team contributions, and trends over time. With detailed breakdowns by category, project, and drawing hierarchy, it allows users to monitor form statuses, such as open, closed, and subcontractor-handled forms. 

  

Key trends and performance metrics, like average status duration and open forms by team, provide actionable insights for identifying bottlenecks and optimizing workflows. 

  

The inclusion of monthly process tracking and visual distribution of status groups ensures comprehensive monitoring to improve team performance and project outcomes.

**Visuals**

**![Company Level Process Overview](/images/kb/103000327419/K7ChLnofOqxQggF2OsfzUuv5wxkxW83hkw.png)**  

  

1.  **Status Groups -** Visual breakdown of forms grouped by status for a simplified overview.
2.  **Embedded Data -** The left table shows a breakdown of processes by category (e.g., Quality, Permits) and their distribution across projects. The right table provides a status breakdown of processes within each project, such as open, subcontractor-handled, or rejected.
3.  **Performance and Status Tracking Visuals** 
    
    -   **Total Forms By Status Group/Status**: Displays the number of forms categorized by their status, including open, closed, rejected, and handled by subcontractors.
        
    -   **Total Open Forms By Created Team**: Highlights the teams responsible for creating the most open forms, showing their contributions.
        
    -   **Average Status Duration By Project (In Days)**: Shows how long, on average, forms stay in a particular status for each project, helping identify delays.
        
    -   **Processes By Month**: Tracks the number of processes created and closed each month, along with the percentage closed, to monitor monthly progress.
