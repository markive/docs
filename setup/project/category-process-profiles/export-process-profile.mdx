### **To Batch download -**

Switch mode from "**Browse**" to "**Select**".

![Export Process profile](/images/kb/103000035119/KbfZu63-iciXRauC8ZRBvLicX6hRBSV7QQ.jpeg)

![Export Process profile](/images/kb/103000035119/VVUiFJoUfR7gBI-qHXpJYSew2GdbYvi04A.jpeg)

Click on the item or press the **select all** / **deselect all** button to select item(s).  
The selected item will be highlighted in grey.

![Export Process profile](/images/kb/103000035119/8Jwjv1WBiFsfiKn9RnhKsHEm1ctP_imOjg.png)

Press the **Download** icon to batch download.

![Export Process profile](/images/kb/103000035119/6ZQt_QLPeSyXa8BFXlPznV9G00xMfAyKGg.jpeg)

* * *

### **To download one by one -**  
Select profile, Click the **⋮** on the top right corner.

Click **Download**.
