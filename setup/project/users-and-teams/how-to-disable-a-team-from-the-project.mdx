---
title: "How to disable a team from the project?"
description: "Click the ≡ menu icon on the top left corner to unfold the Menu, get entry to the Setting > Teams page."
---

Click the **≡** menu icon on the top left corner to unfold the Menu, get entry to the **Setting** > **Teams** page.

![How to disable a team from the project?](/images/kb/103000035143/Vuwhf9_7ikSPPPyVzhT2GjZ-aCWbh9l8aw.jpeg)

![How to disable a team from the project?](/images/kb/103000035143/V5O3UY4HhQeQQvhYFHCnmyAS2yZWZfBn2A.jpeg)

Click the **⋮** button on the right-hand side of the Team name > **Disable**.

![How to disable a team from the project?](/images/kb/103000035143/mEycXCHQO3zkhvBn4YmPDLVmH1vq1T0V3g.jpeg)

![How to disable a team from the project?](/images/kb/103000035143/mKEFFPG2iLBuQdh0rDEntWUsafbpNssO8w.jpeg)

✨You can re-enable the team by [add a team](https://snagr-support.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions/articles/103000035146-how-to-add-team-to-project-) from the company anytime.
