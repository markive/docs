Click the **≡** menu icon on the top left corner to unfold the Menu, get entry to the **Setting** > **Teams** page.

![How to edit team info?](/images/kb/103000035144/x4UGgGBPjjlvrWhBPsYHj2dFBikEzSFiJw.jpeg)

![How to edit team info?](/images/kb/103000035144/YlN9eYY7-HRRhavT5PNrEbe-ZadJl5E_OA.jpeg)

Click the **⋮** button on the right-hand side of the Team name > **Edit**.

![How to edit team info?](/images/kb/103000035144/x8wVnpgaIVcHC0vB2UKYNnyS8yIlEWwhhg.jpeg)

![How to edit team info?](/images/kb/103000035144/2MhLqFnCO6kmx1wM8tv37W4KfQLyVOXfjQ.jpeg)

From here you are able to edit the team's code, name and team profile.  
Edit the info and click **OK** to save.

![How to edit team info?](/images/kb/103000035144/yDcOHalA4PKL3lNes6vhF8lIYP2gl58y_Q.jpeg)
