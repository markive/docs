---
title: "How to upload drawing(s)?"
description: "You can always change and edit drawings."
---

You can always change and edit drawings.

  
Click the **≡** menu icon on the top left corner to unfold the Menu, get entry to the **Settings** > **Drawings** page.

![How to upload drawing(s)?](/images/kb/103000035139/JIo01Fy_sZWqpHrbP6-fQnHZWYEpipswlg.jpeg)

![How to upload drawing(s)?](/images/kb/103000035139/xzKbx4OGAmP1rW_UTxnXgEwBxCfmHVnE2w.jpeg)

Select the drawing set and click the **+** button on the bottom right corner to add drawing(s).

![How to upload drawing(s)?](/images/kb/103000035139/Om7QFDDFmtz9HxKcSA1ElboR8aVjmq8wuQ.jpeg)

You can drag and drop the drawing file or click to select to add.

![How to upload drawing(s)?](/images/kb/103000035139/uB7GU3Ulf7lSo9Tx1oHHsBwkU_lSmyedUw.jpeg)

The process bar will move as shown and lets you know the drawing is uploading.

  

![How to upload drawing(s)?](/images/kb/103000035139/heyijl-Ws3L7nuF1g82uPgsnYH5Qx4pOqQ.gif)

<Info>
The drawing title will same as the uploaded file name.
</Info>
