Click the **≡** menu icon on the top left corner to unfold the Menu, get entry to the **Settings** > **Drawings** page.

![Find and replace the drawings / drawing sets title](/images/kb/103000035135/Pi5KO-t-QP6-5NSzHneSEMNOqR1INkdJig.jpeg)

![Find and replace the drawings / drawing sets title](/images/kb/103000035135/22ZJIODMYlUA_wu8NXIgOniQsHJoqc35eQ.jpeg)

Click the **⋮** button on the right-hand side of the drawing set title > **Find and replace**.

![Find and replace the drawings / drawing sets title](/images/kb/103000035135/BcyiJkdrz_OPNqIplhN9CHOo_z5QKmX98w.jpeg)

![Find and replace the drawings / drawing sets title](/images/kb/103000035135/rJO4xHaKJOq1aj_sw8G_Gih5lUzDB82hWw.jpeg)

Enter the value you want to find and replace, and click below button to replace.

![Find and replace the drawings / drawing sets title](/images/kb/103000035135/HGkFnQ7xPEJ9GbmIoQhlb3pN04Mt1To0lA.jpeg)
