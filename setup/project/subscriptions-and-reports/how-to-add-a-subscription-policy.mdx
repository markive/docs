When adding a **Subscription Policy** it is important to remember that each rule of the subscription policy has different **conditions** and **options** for you to control. these can be combined to suit your requirements.

**via Website**

Simply press the Menu icon ≡ in the top left corner of your screen to unfold the Menu and gain access to the **Subscriptions** page within the **Settings** drop-down as shown below.

![How to add a Subscription Policy?](/images/kb/103000035109/lODWOE1yasreDyQNsXJp9swQhe--qdehrQ.jpeg)

![How to add a Subscription Policy?](/images/kb/103000035109/53Z6Q9stlF1sET6aLhaN4H3Hq42vIJEwEw.jpeg)

Press **+** button circled below to add the subscription policy.

Enter the subscription policy title and click "**OK**" to confirm.

![How to add a Subscription Policy?](/images/kb/103000035109/xvHrzS_Lc1Bjfbw-nE_JkJ9SslmKjCC2rA.jpeg)

![How to add a Subscription Policy?](/images/kb/103000035109/3p-vzkECzCo1XI90HOXY2MsN0EfQGDkDyQ.jpeg)

The next thing to do is to press the **+** button on the bottom right-hand corner of your screen to add **rule(s)**.

The subscription policy rule window will appear in the centre of your screen. here you will need to select whether your subscription policy is in regard to any issues/defects/records or processes/checklists as applicable as shown below.

![How to add a Subscription Policy?](/images/kb/103000035109/LnYe0MnlglrhzZrQ1tbgBdFaGFkQz8Zh_A.jpeg)

![How to add a Subscription Policy?](/images/kb/103000035109/CGFEN2mmtBO53Cbn5ESmTsYKRuVxGTWgIg.jpeg)

You can choose when the **subscription** notification is sent out via the "**When**" option as shown below, this will determine the notifications being sent and at what stage. Selecting "**Issue is under control**" will prompt notifications to be sent out at every stage of the issue/defect/record or process/checklist dependent on the subscription rule applied.

(_Please note that when a subscription is applied, it will determine which notifications are received. ie. If the subscription is set up for subcontractors, the notifications will only be received when the issue/defect/record or process/checklist reaches the stage for the subcontractors to take action_)

![How to add a Subscription Policy?](/images/kb/103000035109/5FhroRX6YX6ECiojKRZJ8RoB0yO1vfgTIA.png)

Adding a "**Condition**" will determine under what parameters the notifications are sent out. Not entering a condition will leave all options open.

![How to add a Subscription Policy?](/images/kb/103000035109/9baF6KND2EpQRPpOphFNTHhK90Lr-E7vkg.png)

![How to add a Subscription Policy?](/images/kb/103000035109/xCpFwQJvYlGtbKvl5Crz1PgatelsXdnh5Q.png)

You can now set the **subscription** to the relevant Team, Team Member(s) or User(s) as required via the "**Add Option**" dropdown.

![How to add a Subscription Policy?](/images/kb/103000035109/WSw3J1iFQfPV8zQrTs1AvmqThqeyeef6sQ.png)

Your subscription is now set-up!
