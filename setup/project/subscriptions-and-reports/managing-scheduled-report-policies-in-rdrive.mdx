### **Overview**

  
_This guide explains how to create, edit, and manage scheduled report policies in RDrive. These reports are automatically sent based on the rules and schedules you define._  
  

-   **Purpose:** To automate the distribution of project data through scheduled reports.
-   **Who It’s For:** Project admin with report management permissions.  
      
      
    

<Note>
Prerequisites: Ensure you have access to the Scheduled Report page and permission to manage scheduled reports.
</Note>

* * *

A **Scheduled Report** allows you to email reports automatically using conditions across both **Issues** and **Processes**.  
Each rule in the policy can be configured with different conditions and filter options.  
Feel free to choose any combination of rules and filters that best fit your project needs. ?

## ✅ **Add a Scheduled Report Policy**

1.  Go to the **Scheduled Reports** page from the side menu **≡** .  
    ![Managing Scheduled Report Policies in RDrive](/images/kb/103000037360/nh6eCh5mdYgM76xyu1nAVa9St7D2BdbKeA.jpeg)  
      
    ![Managing Scheduled Report Policies in RDrive](/images/kb/103000037360/QyfLOKLY2QOxNGsnZh-4RlDfDKf7f8ci9Q.png)  
    
2.  Click the **Add (+)** button to create a new policy.  
    ![Managing Scheduled Report Policies in RDrive](/images/kb/103000037360/9NHxVy2vcSXx8C04j2gRI39WrZ7cwfPHnQ.jpeg)
    
3.  Define the report **frequency**:
    
    -   **Daily**: Select the day(s) and set the delivery time.
        
    -   **Weekly**: Select one or more weekdays and the time.
        
    -   **Monthly**: Choose the time. Reports are sent on the 1st day of each month.![Managing Scheduled Report Policies in RDrive](/images/kb/103000037360/aUqlSxrqeWYER6s0Gwd7MPtzh_7-irZsUQ.jpeg)  
        
4.  Enter a **Report Title** (e.g., “Weekly Progress Summary”).
    
5.  Click **OK** to confirm.  
      
    ![Managing Scheduled Report Policies in RDrive](/images/kb/103000037360/sgcAvfa-2QWLlNDyg06HVX6lPFtSeknsdQ.jpeg)
    
6.  Add **Recipients** by selecting user names, team names, or team tags.
    
7.  Unfold **ISSUE** or **PROCESS** to define rules (see below).  
    ![Managing Scheduled Report Policies in RDrive](/images/kb/103000037360/1eDud7Sv9Wf8GWyySaFu5h0_A7OGaxZq9w.jpeg)

## ✅ **Edit a Schedule**

To edit the schedule of an existing report policy:

1.  Click the **three-dot menu (****︙****)** next to the policy.  
    ![Managing Scheduled Report Policies in RDrive](/images/kb/103000037360/p26w5yapVUJ6krIpvCcnj1zbKClh7_Qo7w.png)
    
2.  Select **Schedule**![Managing Scheduled Report Policies in RDrive](/images/kb/103000037360/-d6FpzNRDWDAFFMgRHpbN8wEX66B2tIcPA.png)
    
3.  Update the report frequency:
    

-   **Daily**: Select the day(s) and set the delivery time.
    
-   **Weekly**: Choose one or more weekdays and specify the time.
    
-   **Monthly**: Set the time. Reports will be sent on the 1st day of each month.
    

Click **OK** to save your changes.  
![Managing Scheduled Report Policies in RDrive](/images/kb/103000037360/JcGrfyRShScp-IT9T4842gdUqHCJcvFZ_A.png)

## **✅ Upload a Scheduled Report Policy (.json file)**

If you have previously downloaded a `.json` file of a scheduled report policy (e.g., for backup or sharing across projects), you can upload it to restore or reuse the settings:

1.  Click the **Add (+)** button to start creating a new policy.
    
2.  At the bottom-right corner of the popup, click the **UPLOAD** button.  
    ![Managing Scheduled Report Policies in RDrive](/images/kb/103000037360/7ORcpWAYLxF9Rhxhit0HTWedmnh_4vKGtA.png)
    
3.  Select the `.json` file from your device.
    

The policy will be uploaded and appear in your policy list.

## **FAQs & Troubleshooting**

### **Q: Why didn’t I receive the scheduled report?**  
A: Please check the following:

-   The scheduled time has passed.
-   You were correctly added as a recipient.
-   Email settings are configured properly.
-   Additionally, check the Email Log to verify if the report was sent.

* * *

### **Q: Can I schedule multiple reports?**

A: Yes, you can create as many report policies as needed, each with its own title, filters, and schedule.

* * *

### **Q: Can I edit or stop a report after it has been scheduled?**

A: Yes. You can open the report policy anytime to edit the title, schedule, recipients, rules, or layout. If you wish to stop the report, you can either delete the policy or remove all recipients to prevent it from being sent.
