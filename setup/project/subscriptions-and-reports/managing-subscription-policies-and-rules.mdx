## **Accessing Subscription Policy Settings via the Website**

1.  Click on the **≡** menu icon at the top left corner to open the Menu.![Managing Subscription Policies and Rules](/images/kb/103000035110/I97GTEv5wi-MDF7ynIJ5WiGh1JyMx1dvjg.png)![Managing Subscription Policies and Rules](/images/kb/103000035110/NHTR-TEPb6L2VTXChMlJDLINoX-fa8zTmw.png)
2.  Navigate to **Settings > Subscriptions** to access the Subscriptions page.

* * *

### **Editing a Subscriptions Policy Title**

1.  Select the desired Subscription policy.
2.  Click on the policy title to edit it.![Managing Subscription Policies and Rules](/images/kb/103000035110/MZIe6gy1sPn0VHOCkQaXfV-Fg2uUJwR6aQ.png)

* * *

## **Downloading a** **Subscription** **Policy**

1.  Click the **⋮** button on the right-hand side of the policy title.**![Managing Subscription Policies and Rules](/images/kb/103000035110/QLjY4ukCH2D-XrZ5OXfqyu1U60zfO4qnYg.png)**
2.  Select **Download**.**![Managing Subscription Policies and Rules](/images/kb/103000035110/oHzmq1_y_XI8NnRocwykCzebDmWSFwL_NQ.png)**

#### **To Batch Download:**

1.  Switch the mode from "**BROWER**" to "**SELECT**".**![Managing Subscription Policies and Rules](/images/kb/103000035110/ujGj6pBH15qK8XhAX5mka_5EXen6xp0TXQ.png)![Managing Subscription Policies and Rules](/images/kb/103000035110/BUyHa_Yv9KYgTiF62pp3UiOQvIZEvZQ9UA.png)**
2.  Click on individual items or use the **Select All / Deselect All** button to choose multiple items. The selected items will be highlighted in grey.**
    
      
    **![Managing Subscription Policies and Rules](/images/kb/103000035110/AVl564bwcii7uFF_axMIah0VzMyupTKgng.png)**
    
    **
3.  Press the **Download** icon to batch download the selected items.**![Managing Subscription Policies and Rules](/images/kb/103000035110/obsMIczqZq81fzj7UK9xoczHyza6YZ7cng.png)**

* * *

## **Deleting a Subscription Policy**

1.  Click the **⋮** button on the right-hand side of the policy title.**![Managing Subscription Policies and Rules](/images/kb/103000035110/zzaY3Yvfs_pOuNMrFTFIMUCdFi8JPlWaVA.png)**
2.  Select **Delete**.**![Managing Subscription Policies and Rules](/images/kb/103000035110/SdOQ1cQb9xAefbTln24cHSNuX6Z2XP08EA.png)**
3.  Confirm the **DELETE**  
    **![Managing Subscription Policies and Rules](/images/kb/103000035110/EtnGKf1eRk1VXl4OQrVe3z_27XlELZx5rg.png)**

> Warning: Once deleted, the Subscription policy cannot be recovered.

* * *

## **Editing Conditions and Options for a Rule**

1.  Click on a rule to expand it.**![Managing Subscription Policies and Rules](/images/kb/103000035110/dBkV6v4TYV7qQ5soE4Dpz2cpiOoMILoscQ.png)**
2.  Press the Add Condition or Add Option button to modify the rule as needed.**![Managing Subscription Policies and Rules](/images/kb/103000035110/U9q4Ydrx2aUF22OlMOZ7rbIjzOrssDW-rg.png)**

* * *

## **Deleting a Rule**

1.  Click the ⋮ button on the right-hand side of the rule.**![Managing Subscription Policies and Rules](/images/kb/103000035110/xsEbK1_hYkEwzqQeUcAzA29AFSZ8kiwxdQ.png)**
2.  Select Delete.**![Managing Subscription Policies and Rules](/images/kb/103000035110/A17ZhqrWXpF55JWt4wf7vbNzWvAf1EgQeA.png)**

* * *

By following these steps, you can effectively manage your subscription policies and rules to suit your specific needs.
