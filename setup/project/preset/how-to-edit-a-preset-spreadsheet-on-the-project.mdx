The sheets in the preset spreadsheet are inserted into the original spreadsheet if merged into the template computation. If the name of the sheet in the preset spreadsheet is the same as the one in the original spreadsheet, the former replaces the latter.  
  
**Editing the preset spreadsheet at the project level**  
Project admin/users with edit Preset permission can make changes on top of the preset spreadsheet that is set on the company level or reset to default to fit the project needs specifically.

* * *

  
**via Website**

Click the **≡** menu icon on the top left corner to unfold the Menu, and get entry to the **Setting** > **Presets** page.

![How to edit a preset spreadsheet on the project?](/images/kb/103000035009/BNdamlsr4zwzNqtZKhX_-sg1blHytdtv9w.jpeg)

![How to edit a preset spreadsheet on the project?](/images/kb/103000035009/2Ga_NnAAn4T-p0kbP38CsNo0OddjsiZsOA.jpeg)

Select **SPREADSHEET.**

![How to edit a preset spreadsheet on the project?](/images/kb/103000035009/7H7lNLOgLYhkWGYSIPW6nvY2DXq-kQhoig.jpeg)

and click on the preset spreadsheet you would like to edit.

![How to edit a preset spreadsheet on the project?](/images/kb/103000035009/h1Sw-M5v93-VgY0kGiH2SOfSmVp65Lgv7A.jpeg)

on this spreadsheet page, you may

-   [Re-upload](#re-upload)
