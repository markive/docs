Team profile serves as the foundation for teams. When you set up teams, you **MUST** pick a team profile which contains different user roles. Please be aware that each team can only be assigned with one team profile.  
  
Each role in a team profile is associated with one or multiple permission policies. This is to set a frame on what users can do on the platform if they are being added to a team and being assigned with a certain role within that team.

* * *

  
Click the **≡** menu icon on the top left corner to unfold the Menu, get entry to the **Setting** > **Team** **profiles** page.

![What is team profile and how to setup?](/images/kb/103000035145/NuIcFK0UsUaBejz2hopig04RIYbXtkz1Vw.jpeg)

![What is team profile and how to setup?](/images/kb/103000035145/OoeCdBX2pgTq5Zn2e_jdsoUsQbs6i7o40g.jpeg)

Press **+** button to add team profile.

![What is team profile and how to setup?](/images/kb/103000035145/alTI-uh4qYAL8bmvWr0mQ1wvwyEXSXbYoQ.jpeg)

Enter the team profile title and click **OK** to confirm.

![What is team profile and how to setup?](/images/kb/103000035145/5ilz7TlAMMRiuwPq9j2GSUkLmWwDb8fX7w.jpeg)

after that, click the **+** button on the bottom right corner to **add a role**, enter the title of the role and select permission policy.
