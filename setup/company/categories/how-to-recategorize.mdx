**[Category](https://snagr-support.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions/articles/103000035055-how-to-add-a-category-and-subcategory-)** is a classification tool for you to manage all the templates.

This is a tutorial to let you know how to recategorize templates into another subcategory.

You may single-edit or batch-edit by templates.

First of all, please access the subcategory template list from the company level to start.

* * *

## **Single**

#### **Issue/record -** 

Select the template and click the **≡** button to expand the menu.

![How to recategorize?](/images/kb/103000035083/gb6oNH3KbUHOiaNMkG5gi47sCyC7vuwThw.jpeg)

Select **RECATEGORIZE**.

![How to recategorize?](/images/kb/103000035083/ND2S3m38ABmTqAb8Vd9HIyRYcq_Sg_2MZw.jpeg)

Choose one subcategory, **OK** to confirm the move.

Done.

![How to recategorize?](/images/kb/103000035083/yArmODeYsd5KNNFWEg5xqZL7r0bbWit6-w.jpeg)

# **Batch-Edit**

### **Issue/record-**

Switch the mode from **Browse** to **Select**.

![How to recategorize?](/images/kb/103000035083/B3YM5F8S2si3EB3IUtJ2bmoZ41UcsNxr8Q.jpeg)

![How to recategorize?](/images/kb/103000035083/-EvT_pnI761EiYH1aCqtFMJOXiNI6Fh6Qg.jpeg)

On the select, you can select multiple templates and click the recategorize icon.

![How to recategorize?](/images/kb/103000035083/F627g_olbc_om_QuHHTZwpLBcQPEtVjahQ.jpeg)

![How to recategorize?](/images/kb/103000035083/pHyNCZ4EQDBwdkYJx-xI0ogc2CQZMtngIg.jpeg)

Choose one subcategory, **OK** to confirm the move.

Done.

![How to recategorize?](/images/kb/103000035083/b4kSFZj5XqdMm4ZYZVd9RtOUK7dSUSg_2A.jpeg)
