Click the **⋮** button next to the Create new version button and select **Download**.

## **Batch download**

Switch the mode from **BROWSE** to **SELECT**.

Press the **Download** icon to batch download.

![How to update the process template & form?](/images/kb/103000035050/t8DyViaP6985zQ_REbP6wqqOCcIzjj0Ieg.jpeg)

## **Rename**

You can [single edit](#single) the template title & template tag(s) on the BROWSE viewer and [batch edit the tag(s)](#batch-edit-tag\(s\)) on the SELECT viewer.

## **Single**

Click the **⋮** button next to the Create new version button and select **Rename**.

![How to update the process template & form?](/images/kb/103000035050/6ehBNWuV_XgRycyThexk-y7FGjxChciuKg.jpeg)

![How to update the process template & form?](/images/kb/103000035050/xp_SKi4YxMQ-t-4jr7lr8Fy7Fa2hXCf2uA.jpeg)

In this dialog, you may edit the title & tag(s) of this template.  
Press **OK** to confirm the change.

![How to update the process template & form?](/images/kb/103000035050/Ed6isUIXppNS8ePp7Nmai2aqic7myDzowQ.jpeg)

## **Batch edit tag(s)**

Switch the mode from **BROWSE** to **SELECT**.

The selected will be highlighted in grey.

![How to update the process template & form?](/images/kb/103000035050/UP1lHu15OR5-cYkE38rmKT3QINxXrHis1g.png)

Press the **rename** icon to batch edit tag(s).

![How to update the process template & form?](/images/kb/103000035050/3L2k8RTxE2q11ewc9FlAYkfEEAmlHL4QpA.jpeg)

**OK** to confirm.

## **Access to form wizard**

Click the button below to access the [form wizard](https://docs.rdrive.io/setup/company/categories/categories-process-template/what-can-i-do-at-the-form-wizard) to edit the form content including form status, fields, etc.

_(no need to add new version if you only need to edit tags)_

![How to update the process template & form?](/images/kb/103000035050/2_ckr-jvcfVC3g1Q7XEZQgqsaEhyvko7Rw.jpeg)

* * *

## **Edit form image**

Click the **⋮** button at the top right corner of the form page, you may [change paper size](#to-change-paper-size), [change page image](#to-change-the-page-image) and [delete the page](#to-delete-page) from here.

![How to update the process template & form?](/images/kb/103000035050/pEOoQXmnWp1-gsz3OfsbucS_N75f8DeGcQ.jpeg)

## **T****o change paper size**

Click **Change paper size** to start.

You may select change the page size by [standard](#standard) or [custom](#custom).

![How to update the process template & form?](/images/kb/103000035050/rnPRaeb_Lce3dy_0q1Vjy4GJ1n2WBUzXhQ.jpeg)

## **Standard**

On Standard, you can select the page **layout** and page **size** to change.  
**OK** to confirm.

![How to update the process template & form?](/images/kb/103000035050/jWtpV-2THfBo9sAEOOWchsMlzAQifn37tw.jpeg)

## **Custom**

On Custom, you can according to the sample text size adjust the page size by zooming in & out.

**OK** to confirm.

![How to update the process template & form?](/images/kb/103000035050/O0I8bk7fTFbjCOuDzQhOwfNDmtJnJUqdpQ.jpeg)

## **To change the page image**

Click **Change page image** to start.

![How to update the process template & form?](/images/kb/103000035050/EB03oaLkMgSW5Q0DJe97J-mtvmWa4FEtEA.jpeg)

The upload window will pop up for you to upload the image for the replacement.

![How to update the process template & form?](/images/kb/103000035050/_6phNfkvxmJWcs2w_NPXASBP937nuXlrEw.jpeg)

## **To delete page**

Click the **Delete** button will delete that page immediately.

![How to update the process template & form?](/images/kb/103000035050/23hUODQap6Ay-nrXfvYtMMQF5pOojoEzpw.jpeg)

## **Edit form info**

Select **Edit** to bring up the form information dialogue to edit the Form **Title**, **Tags**, **Description**, **Language**, and Default or **Optional** setting.

**OK** to confirm.

![How to update the process template & form?](/images/kb/103000035050/TWG9y-7HYY1FXEjbKtV1aZDQ6H0R6ydjuA.jpeg)

## **Rules**

The assignment rule set from the company area will automatically appear to all project process profiles.

Select **Rules** to open the dialogue.

![How to update the process template & form?](/images/kb/103000035050/fFUVYdvXNLGEvZIyQ60JRFpWV7zs-h97cg.jpeg)

Press the **ADD** button to add a new rule to configure this form or click on the existing **rule** to edit.

![How to update the process template & form?](/images/kb/103000035050/ZRb6UIiV5l7GbbD3F_HNNGFtC9C1dA3O1Q.jpeg)

![How to update the process template & form?](/images/kb/103000035050/J4rOsPcLYvPKemb1G4pDxijEDsQe3o5cLw.jpeg)

Select Form status, assign attributes, and Form data if needed.  
**OK** to confirm.

![How to update the process template & form?](/images/kb/103000035050/EmBxqAxaTWDlQgGNmnSivtt0ARMvQ4-2HQ.jpeg)

For more details of the assignment rule, please reference [HERE](https://docs.rdrive.io/setup/project/category-process-profiles/assignment-rule-process-profile).

## **Reorder**

To reorder the form sorting, please click **Reorder.**  
Drag and drop the form to reorder at the pop-up window.

**OK** to confirm.
