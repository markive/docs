##   
  
**1\. Creating Fields![What can I do at the form wizard?](/images/kb/103000035051/8T3xR_472TfXiRH2bkEHYkI7XlvjBtvV7w.png)**

-   **Select Fields**: Click the corresponding icon (or use ⌘/Ctrl 1) to select fields. ( **☩ )**
-   **Create Fields (Manual)**: Click this icon (or use ⌘/Ctrl 2) to manually create fields.
-   **Create Fields (Edge Detection)**: Click this icon (or use ⌘/Ctrl 3) to create fields based on edge detection.

**Example Use Cases:![What can I do at the form wizard?](/images/kb/103000035051/iwr_rPPpo1G-NJqbfj9mD63Szg3EvKvaHA.png)**

-   **Select Hotspots**: Click this icon to multi-select fields and make batch changes.
    
    Examples:
    
    -   Copy (⌘/Ctrl C) and paste (⌘/Ctrl V) fields from one page/form to another.
    -   Update the data type of fields.
    -   Modify field titles.
    -   Link checkboxes.

**_Note_**_: Edge detection may fail if lines are not clear or well-defined. Use the Move tool (⌘/Ctrl 7) to adjust edge sensitivity as needed.  
![What can I do at the form wizard?](/images/kb/103000035051/-shw2J3qL3XwLy7DFer0x8zKUKcZNyvEwg.png)_

_You can also use the page list and zoom options to adjust the viewer while setting up the fields.__![What can I do at the form wizard?](/images/kb/103000035051/zhwzgjBwV2YvSX2AIzJMXnbLqtewmthQXA.png)![What can I do at the form wizard?](/images/kb/103000035051/pFhMBvc2MExmqJ7sbWe6DkJcj80p2KgRDQ.png)_

Click the icon (or use ⌘/Ctrl 4) to open the field properties.[![What can I do at the form wizard?](/images/kb/103000035051/qn_5b9RJTobrJnJKeAJjVoUD2yvbJygJbw.png)](https://intercom.help/rdrive/en/articles/5938354-field-properties)

In the **[Field Properties](https://docs.rdrive.io/setup/company/categories/categories-template-setup-knowledge-base/what-can-field-properties-do)** panel, you can:

![What can I do at the form wizard?](/images/kb/103000035051/1JiF8mYAq6PnxKz1ffAwaAjLDtzN8RcdjA.png)  

-   Name the fields in the **Attribute** section.

## **3\. Setting the Form Status**

To manage the form status, click the **Form Statuses** option (⌘/Ctrl 6) in the more tools list. This will open the form status dialogue, allowing company administrators to manage statuses for the form.![What can I do at the form wizard?](/images/kb/103000035051/70qNth_khkmBHmPIbZPcyaJ7CFFtbaNIUQ.png)

By default, three statuses are provided:

-   Draft
-   Open
-   Closed

#### **\-** **Add**  

To add a new status, click the **ADD** button in the bottom right corner. You can then:![What can I do at the form wizard?](/images/kb/103000035051/CZWIGzBgw6AVgSokz4uF2qSItcy6veVO1w.png)

-   Select the status stage.
-   Choose a color to represent the status.
-   Enter a title and [add status tags.](https://docs.rdrive.io/faq/setup/how-are-tags-used)
-   Adjust [conditional editing settings.](https://docs.rdrive.io/faq/setup/how-are-tags-used)

Click **OK** to confirm.

#### **\- Delete**  
If necessary, you can delete an existing status by selecting it and clicking **DELETE**.![What can I do at the form wizard?](/images/kb/103000035051/24AcenOTJaSArg0Hu6w1OWW6nwyU30I3cQ.png)![What can I do at the form wizard?](/images/kb/103000035051/occwzrOqb6lvcOYzUaiGMRCsLnQ2OWu_Pg.png)

#### **\- Edit**

Additionally, you can edit any status as required by clicking into it, making changes, and saving.![What can I do at the form wizard?](/images/kb/103000035051/yPq_HdnBY6I1SDOlqFcAAUjOZIfTpXpkng.png)![What can I do at the form wizard?](/images/kb/103000035051/G4r7jAh5QezK_-ogypC66Doqn7vA1GCF0A.png)

#### **\- Exit**

To exit the dialogue, click outside the box.  
![What can I do at the form wizard?](/images/kb/103000035051/YwPfyz7DMPQ-P8lOOBN2jgaMLT2MAzVMZg.png)

## **4\. Saving Changes**

Always remember to save your changes by clicking the **SAVE** button.

Once all changes are finalized, you can return to the template page to [publish](https://docs.rdrive.io/setup/company/categories/categories-process-template/create-a-process-template-with-form) the form as a new version.![What can I do at the form wizard?](/images/kb/103000035051/_6zqbKCf20icTe9eLsLfNPB_98i0x_bR2Q.png)
