This article shows you how to manage the [created cover templates](https://docs.rdrive.io/setup/company/cover-templates/create-a-report-cover-template).

You may [Edit](#Edit), [Download](#Download---single-&-batch), and [Delete](#Delete) from this page.

  

  

First, log in to **Company Area**.

  

<Info>
Only the company admin can access this page.
</Info>

  

Click **Settings** and select **Cover templates**.

![Manage the cover templates](/images/kb/103000035041/eiaPKydwM4qRXYE-c1FqCaa0AdhVs6Ppng.jpeg)

![Manage the cover templates](/images/kb/103000035041/Xk7abjylvIN-XSRyPpGkqWlvXKhzNpLN1w.jpeg)

* * *

## **Edit**

Click the **⋮** button and select **Edit**.

![Manage the cover templates](/images/kb/103000035041/UIN7yvgnztXa1LiJoNmCtWS3XSZX3hcP7A.jpeg)

![Manage the cover templates](/images/kb/103000035041/l7iswXuYCFAJB3Mfs3B8i5wTGjUFljAGsw.jpeg)

In this dialog, you may edit the title, type, orientation, and background image of this template.  
Press **OK** to confirm the change.

![Manage the cover templates](/images/kb/103000035041/ADsPDRRvRQBlLtSOJeQY4KmsiiMEr1_GJw.jpeg)

* * *

## Download - [single](#Single-download) & [batch](#Batch-download)

-   **Single download**

Click the **⋮** button and select **Download**.

![Manage the cover templates](/images/kb/103000035041/4M6AXNLjlyZZL4EaXqCi6Zw4-TdfDrgRog.jpeg)

![Manage the cover templates](/images/kb/103000035041/pVqtvMLNw2ToHMcQzTm83OiSALWUkL31PQ.jpeg)

-   **Batch download**

Switch the mode from **BROWSE** to **SELECT**.

![Manage the cover templates](/images/kb/103000035041/2CtWv1w2fcTzw2htJK0xoMdQY6KxdN09vA.jpeg)

![Manage the cover templates](/images/kb/103000035041/ZIZWWNQlhs7uSXLbVWj28C-PVW_bRfj8gg.jpeg)

On the SELECT, you can click on the template or press the **select all** / **deselect all** button to select item(s).  
The selected item will be highlighted in grey.

![Manage the cover templates](/images/kb/103000035041/ITBrnFnAKV49Gyl4qZhwGISvJcQTey21HQ.png)

Press the **Download** icon to start batch download.

![Manage the cover templates](/images/kb/103000035041/orOzQJHRB_Ur8YzC9clacFcemeR26khhGw.jpeg)

* * *

## Delete

Click the **⋮** button, click **Delete** to request delete template.

![Manage the cover templates](/images/kb/103000035041/AhxtVAr9h8jGIoacIfmUClqpBXUN57ijSQ.jpeg)

![Manage the cover templates](/images/kb/103000035041/4EJmbaOaKEJJku6pvhMTseluUSvQi7i8PQ.jpeg)

**DELETE** again to confirm the delete.
