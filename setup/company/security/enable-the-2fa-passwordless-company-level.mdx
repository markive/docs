---
title: "Enable the 2FA/Passwordless (Company Level)"
description: "Two-Factor Authentication/Passwordless can only be enabled by the company administrator on the company level."
---

  
  
Two-Factor Authentication/Passwordless can only be enabled by the company administrator on the company level.

  

Login to **Company Admin Area**.

Click the **≡** Menu icon at the top left corner to unfold the Menu, go to the **Security**  > **Authentication** page.

![Enable the 2FA/Passwordless (Company Level)](/images/kb/103000035149/eciRod01NKPYFb4d-3JYr-qb0VgR8TVccA.jpeg)

![Enable the 2FA/Passwordless (Company Level)](/images/kb/103000035149/Iz5Gzf1AojmlzGjg5rA_SJU8QTf-q1oppQ.jpeg)

Click **Authentication type** and select the type you want.

It will save automatically in a few seconds.

![Enable the 2FA/Passwordless (Company Level)](/images/kb/103000035149/I9xntiILbaiP9vQa5jhakgjJVmUJI1UFRw.jpeg)

![Enable the 2FA/Passwordless (Company Level)](/images/kb/103000035149/qPza6-ZSvVcxBGIc4UzGQ_9cdsLJ_UvxOg.jpeg)

Once it is enabled, users from different projects under this company enterprise have to login with two authentication factors/passwordless.
