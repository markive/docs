**User list** is the list of people who will be able to access RDrive.

## **What is required to set up a user? -** **Display name and email address**

The email address of a user will be used for account login and notifications. If the email address is invalid, the user will fail to receive email notifications from RDrive.

Once everything is ready, click the **≡** Menu icon at the top left corner to unfold the Menu, go to the **Settings** > **Users** page.

Basically, there are two ways to add a user to the Company Level- creating manually or importing a user Excel template.

-   [2️⃣ Importing a user excel template](#2%ef%b8%8f%e2%83%a3-importing-a-user-excel-template)

## 1️⃣ **Adding users manually**

Click the + button.

![How to add user(s) to the Company Level](/images/kb/103000035088/4zLBo6XMSdSXjEibS9x3aSi7bvjrPqOjvg.jpg)

Enter the Email address, Display name, Password and Language of the new user.  
You may click the **+** button to add more user if needed, and click OK once done.

![How to add user(s) to the Company Level](/images/kb/103000035088/cWdVcpRFgxwUmNR2driIdva6Y3vab9QCTA.JPG)

## [2️⃣](https://intercom.help/rdrive/en/articles/4679148-how-to-add-user-s-to-project#2-add-company-users-to-project) **Importing a user excel template**

**This is a quicker way to add multiple users.**

Click the + button.

![How to add user(s) to the Company Level](/images/kb/103000035088/4zLBo6XMSdSXjEibS9x3aSi7bvjrPqOjvg.jpg)

Click the **Upload** button at the bottom of the dialogue.

![How to add user(s) to the Company Level](/images/kb/103000035088/Lt3STQdZgBY4zJwrF1fHS80v3ET4rexdRA.JPG)

Download the Excel **template**.

![How to add user(s) to the Company Level](/images/kb/103000035088/jCoH4xKwkExDxgJ8HfLmAE5c2StHVzgagg.jpg)

Enter the Email address, Display name, Password and Language (locale) then save.

![How to add user(s) to the Company Level](/images/kb/103000035088/5sdui5KMhE9bzUgama7Nct8p8McYRQ2ioQ.JPG)

Upload the completed template.

![How to add user(s) to the Company Level](/images/kb/103000035088/etAIvOLn6EO0ZZ6dJ22Umft0xci6OkuvPw.JPG)

Click **OK** to confirm.

![How to add user(s) to the Company Level](/images/kb/103000035088/DfT1WUyw8GSZPyYz8XchCQ6gf6Jgp69Xxg.JPG)
