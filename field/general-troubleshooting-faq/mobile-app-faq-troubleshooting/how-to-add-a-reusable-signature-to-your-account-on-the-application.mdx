### **Overview**

_How to add a reusable signature to your account within the app_

-   **Purpose:** Adding a digital signature within the app to reuse on items within RDrive.
-   **Who It’s For:** General users

<Note>
Prerequisites: RDrive Access
</Note>

  

* * *

## **Step-by-Step Instructions**

### **✅ Step 1: Accessing your Account Settings**

1.  Once logged into the app, click your name at the top of the screen![How to add a reusable signature to your account on the Application](/images/kb/103000399078/smntx7TMNBUCl1UzSAQFud89xH6qIYrmWg.png)
2.  From the preference tab, click into the signature box or press the pen icon to add your signature.![How to add a reusable signature to your account on the Application](/images/kb/103000399078/dBfjnPX5biWPd0mQy85zRRyzE-C8xRXWcQ.png)
3.  Add your signature by dragging on the screen then click **"Save"** in the top left corner. You will now be able to use this signature repeatedly from now on.![How to add a reusable signature to your account on the Application](/images/kb/103000399078/dFfg6FWRgrsSiKPYAet23fcMdJPH25S4tw.png)
