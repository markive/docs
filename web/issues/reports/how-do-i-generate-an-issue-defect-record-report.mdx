### **Overview**

_A brief introduction explaining the purpose of this guide._

-   **Purpose:** Generating issue/defect/record reports
-   **Who It’s For:** General users

<Note>
Prerequisites: Able to read issue/defect/record
</Note>

The first thing you may want to know about RDrive issue reports is that we now fully support **[customised report layout](https://docs.rdrive.io/setup/company/categories/categories-issue-record-template/design-an-issue-record-report-template).** 

This means that we not only provide a standard template, but also enables the capacity and flexibility to create multiple report templates to suit the specific needs of different stakeholders.

If you have your specific requirements/ideas for the report layout, please contact your company/project admin.

## **Via Plan Viewer**

Simply press the Menu icon ≡ in the top left corner of your screen to unfold the Menu and gain access to the Plan Viewer. Then you select the correct drawing.

![How do I generate an issue/defect/record report?](/images/kb/103000035219/uXnKUrZBknGpgonmpOgORsAlt2D4BAGB2g.png)

![How do I generate an issue/defect/record report?](/images/kb/103000035219/PZHExSlY8PEPB9EoabOXuxN1KXFzdAZbXQ.png)

Choose your issue/defect/record pin as circled below. 

Once selected, the details will display on the upper left-hand side of your screen. You will then need to press the 3 dots ⋮ button at the top right corner of the detail display.

![How do I generate an issue/defect/record report?](/images/kb/103000035219/DsDZbxb3NwHXQptd5vRWLx5RGr-Z9dmTSA.png)

![How do I generate an issue/defect/record report?](/images/kb/103000035219/_sbbim7wNz6LhjVcaLjlo0cSb6Y8OYePkw.png)

Once the Menu has unfolded, press "**Download**" and the download options for this issue/defect/record will be enlarged on your screen within the window as shown below.

Select your desired report template from the window options. All you need do now is click "**Download**" to download your report.

![How do I generate an issue/defect/record report?](/images/kb/103000035219/g3ni9hF74vLCuTAsYTja96qj5avhi9LDeA.png)

From here this will display the download window, select **"Advanced"** to see additional download options and configuration.

![How do I generate an issue/defect/record report?](/images/kb/103000035219/XT3tK6MceVIzAogXCsnKWPYDL5fjFI80hA.png)

Make any selections required the click "Download".

![How do I generate an issue/defect/record report?](/images/kb/103000035219/dRz1Nxy7foHOHFuuXboPY8bjiw0rrMekeA.png)

If you have a custom issue report template in place, you can select this as circled below to run your report.

![How do I generate an issue/defect/record report?](/images/kb/103000035219/gFSwws7bxo65v-ViWaiJ661tKO1nDD-sLg.png)

## **To Batch Download**

To download a batch of multiple issues/defects/records, you must first switch the mode from "**Browse**" to "**Select**" as shown below. 

When in "**Select**" mode, you can select multiple issues to export the report as necessary.

![How do I generate an issue/defect/record report?](/images/kb/103000035219/yBSo7QFJuGAJW5mijRnfYseNd8Gas5d_qA.png)

![How do I generate an issue/defect/record report?](/images/kb/103000035219/3Ztussvy9g0A51XOoIXQ5HUG5GyWeOG3pw.png)

You also have the option of refining your download whereby you can filter and search the relevant issues/defects/records.

This can be done via the search bar located at the top of your screen as shown below.

![How do I generate an issue/defect/record report?](/images/kb/103000035219/gQTSyPhvlFQ-YNmhEgviMwhRF4GNJLC2iA.png)

Press the desired issue(s)/defect(s)/record(s) on the left-hand side of your screen, the selected issue(s)/defect(s)/record(s) will be highlighted in grey as shown below. Once you are happy with the issues/defects/records selected, press the "**Download**" button highlighted in the image below.

If you wish to batch download all of the issues/defects/records listed on the left-hand side of your screen there is an even easier way to do this. This can be done by pressing the "**Select All**" button circled below (left) followed by the "**Download**" button highlighted below.

Pressing the "**Deselect All**" button circled below (right) will remove all chosen selections listed.

![How do I generate an issue/defect/record report?](/images/kb/103000035219/CMV6adNoUBixT8OSO2fEqe0ZbCXqSapIpg.png)

![How do I generate an issue/defect/record report?](/images/kb/103000035219/3vGykPYYyc8YQ0cmqx1oVyBen_Gf6H9fqw.png)

![How do I generate an issue/defect/record report?](/images/kb/103000035219/QrUR2EfbBS43S8vWAFmwqxwPhi7czUqgGQ.png)

As above, the option is available to use your custom issue report template if in place. You can select this as circled below to run your report.

![How do I generate an issue/defect/record report?](/images/kb/103000035219/agOcAndKcRclxF7hvEWzJT6r5E_CMQiSqg.png)
