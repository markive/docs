### **Overview**

_How to view items linked to issues/defects/records_

-   **Purpose:** Viewing linked items within RDrive
-   **Who It’s For:** General Users

<Note>
Prerequisites: Read Access
</Note>

  

**Via Issue Finder**

  

Simply press the Menu icon ≡ on the top left corner to unfold the Menu.![How to view the issue/defect/record's linked items](/images/kb/103000035020/vQ8QCR7SSXKgK_BFKl2PdTPKUfGg9QNZsQ.png)

You can now access to the Issue page by selecting the category and subcategory as shown below.  
![How to view the issue/defect/record's linked items](/images/kb/103000035020/SyTsnNfeMJ6a8wqFglUPj6TuWqseF39Lrg.png)

Then click the **≡** button on the right-hand side of your screen to expand the menu.

![How to view the issue/defect/record's linked items](/images/kb/103000035020/3QGVlClTT252DRBcwQgi9L3drYWUy7rDVw.png)

  

Select "**Links**" via the "**View**" option as circled/arrowed below. Once chosen, the details will enlarge in a window on your 

screen.

  

![How to view the issue/defect/record's linked items](/images/kb/103000035020/3Sgk4moJ30QH7x3K6t86coMAfg_RtTfU2Q.png)

  

Select your desired "**I****tems**" from the category and subcategory. this may be "**Links**" and/or "**References**".

  

![How to view the issue/defect/record's linked items](/images/kb/103000035020/Tx59i0Wj0LOD6Dc6Gu1XRptVRwqcBUH59w.png)

  

Choose as required to view the linked items will be shown with the detail window.

  

Press on the desired item to open the issue/defect/record within the Plan Viewer.

  

![How to view the issue/defect/record's linked items](/images/kb/103000035020/7tby739JREjZEQhlbFW8HVhK1oVOY2EZ8w.png)

![How to view the issue/defect/record's linked items](/images/kb/103000035020/lQI41i4tN6gNntOaQrGU0wmyODHczDvU_w.png)

  

**Customise the display of the detail?**

  

We know every user has their own preference. For this reason, RDrive supports customising your own display list!

  
Please click the "Settings" **⚙️** to design the display to your liking or specific requirements.
