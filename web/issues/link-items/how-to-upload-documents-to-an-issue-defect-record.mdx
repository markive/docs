### **Overview**

_How to raise an issue, defect or record using RDrive_

-   **Purpose:** How to Link documents to an issue, defect or record against a location on the app and desktop
-   **Who It’s For:** General users

<Note>
Prerequisites: Able to raise an issue, defect or record
</Note>

It is common that different supporting documents come along with the issue/defect/record itself.

Not to worry, **RDrive** allows you to attach document(s) to your issue/defect/record!  
  

**How to attach documentation:**

**You will NOT be able to attach any document(s) to the issue/defect/record when it's status is set to Closed****!** (_Sometimes, attaching documents will only be possible at a certain status, dependent on the bespoke settings)_

## **via Issue Finder**

To begin, press the Menu ≡ button in the top left corner to unfold the Menu and gain access to the issue/defect/record page by selecting the category and subcategory.

Once you have chosen your issue/defect/record, press the Menu ≡  button on the right-hand side of your screen  as circled below to expand the menu and select "**View"** then **"Links".**

![How to upload documents to an issue/defect/record?](/images/kb/103000035024/hWm_k-gK5ElB0yciubhh3FgNDb3uE3ATXA.png)

![How to upload documents to an issue/defect/record?](/images/kb/103000035024/g_Csxq6kpiRO5oFZ0BM1QNfe09fM2_CV4w.png)

The linked items will be shown if any are available. For the purpose of this example, we will click on "**Linked files**" to add documents.

![How to upload documents to an issue/defect/record?](/images/kb/103000035024/ReQimZbXFXECxeDz9vcPLjVzyKWz9FQJCg.png)

From here we can then upload documents directly from your device or machine by clicking **"Upload documents"** or select documents already uploaded to the Document Repository by clicking **"Select from document repository"**.

![How to upload documents to an issue/defect/record?](/images/kb/103000035024/fHX_yJzaKMdG5PvoejVo-43e-hbuN6Jjzg.png)

Now you can upload the document(s) from your desktop or device either by clicking and selecting the file from your documents which will appear under **"New documents"**, once complete hit **"Done"**.

![How to upload documents to an issue/defect/record?](/images/kb/103000035024/x_w8MauCwYs3XjuTnmqfoqhEECSrUGm3_g.png)

The uploaded document(s) will now show as below as "Linked Files", clicking into this will then show this and allow you to download any documents here.

![How to upload documents to an issue/defect/record?](/images/kb/103000035024/nS3kr0vCxKTYnS1jBmqvWqvGFiATfsqscQ.png)

Your documentation has now been added to your issue/defect/record to view and download any time.

![How to upload documents to an issue/defect/record?](/images/kb/103000035024/1If7XSC8qOP0PHJh2MFKRnQV-EiwhLh28A.png)

Exit this window by clicking outside of the Panel or pressing the Esc key to close.

![How to upload documents to an issue/defect/record?](/images/kb/103000035024/eMAqYv-qc5xhngC86GyuC2JwHqoEduRUpw.png)
