# **Process Status**

Process status is a combination of workflow states of all its forms with the goal of displaying the most recent change to its forms.

## **Single-form Process**

The process status is the same as the workflow state of its one and only form. This effectively makes single-form processes appear the same as forms.

  

![How does a Process/Checklist display its workflow status?](/images/kb/103000035093/dCLfwLVLy8g45tWznWktZIx40aN52HlhWA.png)

  

## **Multi-form Process**

## 1\. The workflow states of all its forms have the same name

The process status is the same as the workflow state of all its forms.

  

![How does a Process/Checklist display its workflow status?](/images/kb/103000035093/XTFd0md0nQmNCW3URwpCNR4Okwp8bSJUDQ.png)

  

## 2\. The most recent non-closed-passed workflow state of one of its form is the initial state

The process status displays the initial state.

  

![How does a Process/Checklist display its workflow status?](/images/kb/103000035093/yT0pkAL_PiQ5wey1nKif9Ud04wkLYZi2og.png)

  

The process status becomes open if it progresses to the next form normally.

  

## 3\. Other combinations of workflow states

The process status displays the name and state of the form with the most recent change in workflow that is not closed-passed.

  

![How does a Process/Checklist display its workflow status?](/images/kb/103000035093/G1-osxbE2Vi6FKGmSHBNhOMfjtjHQwBfug.png)

  

**In general, the process status displays the most recent change that requires attention.**
