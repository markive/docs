If you have a form with added external reference document(s) [](https://intercom.help/rdrive/en/articles/5987980-form-reference-document)you will need to know how and where to find them.

  

Simply press the Menu icon ≡ in the top left corner of your screen to unfold the Menu and access the Process/Checklist page by selecting the category and subcategory as shown below.

  

![Where to view the Form Reference document(s)?](/images/kb/103000035079/GQUtS2FpSf49xx69ujI4J92gNRftDeenrA.jpeg)

![Where to view the Form Reference document(s)?](/images/kb/103000035079/I7dMjzJL0h9bq_s-0-LjjFCm65QbTE37_A.jpeg)

  

Choose your process/checklist to open your form viewer.

  

![Where to view the Form Reference document(s)?](/images/kb/103000035079/BLWwiFkvFcV9Lnb3arzmRAETkxYaIlZYvg.jpeg)

  

Click the 3dots ⋮  button as circled below to unfold the form Menu. 

  

Once unfolded, press Reference and the share dialog of this form will appear as a window in the centre of your screen. The reference documentation will be listed in the window if any are already attached.
