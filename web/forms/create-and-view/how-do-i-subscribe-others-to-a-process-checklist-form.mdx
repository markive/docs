### **Overview**

_How to subscribe users to a process_

-   **Purpose:** Subscribing others to processes within RDrive using the desktop/browser
-   **Who It’s For:** General users

<Note>
Prerequisites: Able to read and share the item, If you want to view/edit the subscriber list you will need a "Share" permission to view the subscriber list.
</Note>

  

Subscribing provides **notifications**. If you have read access, you can subscribe yourself to receive notifications.

  

When subscribing, a user will simultaneously become a **participant**, meaning they will always be able to 1. read and 2. receive notification when there is a status change whatever process/checklist they are subscribing to.

  

If you want to view/edit the subscriber list you will need a "**Share**" permission to view the subscriber list.  
  

# **via Plan Viewer**

Press the Menu icon ≡ in the top left corner of your screen to unfold the Menu and access the Plan viewer. 

  

![How do I Subscribe others to a Form?](/images/kb/103000035173/WSjxW-9MeDSx_UFZioirUnEsi9m9rqA6OQ.png)

  

Press the "**location based item**" circled above to unfold the process/checklist details box on the left-hand side of your screen. then simply select a form and the form viewer will be opened.

![How do I Subscribe others to a Form?](/images/kb/103000035173/oqlSGLaN51QGuy3EsE7OA1jQMUIc7inJBg.png)

  

Click the dropdown menu circled below.

  

![How do I Subscribe others to a Form?](/images/kb/103000035173/PDPSPvgSGjNDBV3EASPy7M94VyzCnYuvIA.png)

  

Once pressed, select "**Subscribe**" from the unfolded menu options.

  

![How do I Subscribe others to a Form?](/images/kb/103000035173/gkFR_EgcMsji3gc6OyohlqC02r8l7ZnPdw.png)

  

Here you can add/remove user(s) from the subscriber list.

  

![How do I Subscribe others to a Form?](/images/kb/103000035173/MzyREd37YuAQiVeq1k2d-lvMxg4eAmLRew.png)

  

All finished? Simply press outside of the subscribe dialogue window to complete.
