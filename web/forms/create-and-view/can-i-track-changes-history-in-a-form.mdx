In **RDrive**, you can track the editing history of the form and also the person responsible for any changes made to the form throughout it's history!  
  
To track the changes/history of a form, you will first have to visit the relevant form. Once on the form page, simply press the Menu icon ≡ in the upper right corner of your screen to unfold the Menu and gain access to the Menu options. 

Select the "**History**" via the "**View**" option on the unfolded Menu as shown below.

![Can I track Changes/History in a form?](/images/kb/103000035208/WYIC6cOiHSuMwPqE-mxS9p7TL5cV_5Dt8A.jpeg)

![Can I track Changes/History in a form?](/images/kb/103000035208/nNGZvcLdvx70hK9_bT44_rwPbVhhu4Wldw.png)

  
You will see a history timeline at the bottom of your screen as shown below.

  
Each dot on the timeline represents a timestamp. Click on every dot to see the changes made/the person who made the changes.

![Can I track Changes/History in a form?](/images/kb/103000035208/JwpquABB4Mhk5DwyudBJQdzBWccKiLazHA.png)

To close the form history timeline, just simply click the dropdown menu again and select "**Close History**"

![Can I track Changes/History in a form?](/images/kb/103000035208/cQLIygzllZEkE6gdiIC2iUokqQJY-OA7rQ.png)
