### **Overview**

_How to access the history of a form while viewing on the web_

-   **Purpose:** See and access a processes history timeline
-   **Who It’s For:** General users

<Note>
Prerequisites: Able to read the item
</Note>

  

  
In **RDrive**, you can track the editing history of the form and also the person responsible for any changes made to the form throughout it's history.  
  
To track the changes/history of a form, you will first have to navigate to the relevant form. Once on the form page, simply press the 3 dot menu in the upper right corner of your screen to unfold the Menu.

  

![Can I track Changes/History in a form?](/images/kb/103000035208/EW7S3JvNpv38szAJxM4Q-LyyUgigcyGqAg.png)

  

Select the "**History**" via the "**View**" option on the unfolded Menu as shown below.

  

![Can I track Changes/History in a form?](/images/kb/103000035208/lxacEtcaXXD3xwf9eUBLFRmZjPeQ2qSDkg.png)

  
You will see a history timeline at the bottom of your screen as shown below.

  
Each dot on the timeline represents a timestamp. Click on every dot to see the changes made/the person who made the changes.

  
**Please note: Changes in the history will be highlighted in blue as you move through the history dots showing what was added any who by at each stage.**  
  

![Can I track Changes/History in a form?](/images/kb/103000035208/f3WQdQc68GsLzko3M9yRL8ikqlcmtIgpJA.png)

  

To close the form history timeline, just simply click the dropdown menu again and select "**Close History**"

  

![Can I track Changes/History in a form?](/images/kb/103000035208/6Wmrhll3YguatpkyFBb1WpnK6VtddwQQfQ.png)
