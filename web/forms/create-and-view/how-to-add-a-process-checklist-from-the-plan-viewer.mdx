##   
**Raising a process/checklist from the "add item" option**

  

Press the ✚ button at the bottom right corner.

  

![How to add a Process/Checklist from the Plan Viewer?](/images/kb/103000035217/EYpOGCx_MdwflbEjC_H6NTrJ0SambN1ZXQ.png)

  

Under the Item section, select a subcategory from the categories list as shown below.

  

![How to add a Process/Checklist from the Plan Viewer?](/images/kb/103000035217/lJ2K1pz9pQ3OZYBn01N_HBQMjvobBlfLCA.png)

  

You will then need to pick the blue location pin in the area you wish to raise your process/checklist.

  

![How to add a Process/Checklist from the Plan Viewer?](/images/kb/103000035217/9tDyENyjDCUyU1phc7zzrVxizRfBLqNYhQ.png)

  

![How to add a Process/Checklist from the Plan Viewer?](/images/kb/103000035217/yf_DQqIcaPZm3_tfAqrO5jbWbXKtTDQ5tw.png)

  

Choose Profile from the subcategory, enter External reference information if necessary and press "**OK"** to confirm.

  

* * *

**Can't see the process you want to add?** 

  

It may be the case that you do not have the permission to create that process/checklist. Please reach out to your Project Administrator for more details.

  
  

### **FAQs & Troubleshooting**

**Q: Can I add multiple processes or checklists from the Plan Viewer?**  
**A:** Yes. Depending on your project setup and permissions, you can add multiple processes or checklists directly from the Plan Viewer.

**Q: Why can’t I see the option to add a process/checklist in the Plan Viewer?**  
**A:** The option may be restricted by your user permissions or project configuration. Ensure you have the correct access rights and that the relevant plan has been loaded correctly.

**Q: Can I add a process/checklist to a specific location on the plan?**  
**A:** Yes. When using the Plan Viewer, you can place the process or checklist at a precise location on the drawing or plan for easier tracking and management.
