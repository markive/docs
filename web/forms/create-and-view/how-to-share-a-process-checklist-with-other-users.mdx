Sharing gives **ad-hoc read access** to other users. You can choose individual users to share with.  
  
Let's say there are users who have edit rights only. If you share a form with them, these users who have edit rights but not read access will be able to edit after you have shared with them. Those who are shared with are called **participants**.  
  
To put it simply, you can share a form with other users to give them permission to see the form(s). (_You will need "**Share**" permissions to perform the "**Share**" action_).

# Share by Form

## **via Plan Viewer**

Simply press the Menu icon ≡ in the top left corner of your screen to unfold the Menu and choose Plan Viewer. 

You can also use the Process unified search bar at the top of your screen to search the forms.

![How to share a Process/Checklist with other users?](/images/kb/103000035185/wVHJIv-kARypKffk2L_g8-g0dkNA0uEhPQ.jpeg)

![How to share a Process/Checklist with other users?](/images/kb/103000035185/M5eDrCrHKmkPhu0CIaW0s_CWRrg4xp991A.jpeg)

Click the memo icon as circled below to open the process details box on the left-hand side of your screen. Once unfolded, select the relevant form and the form viewer will be opened on your screen.

![How to share a Process/Checklist with other users?](/images/kb/103000035185/4FSYQlZov8dpWpfltNNKuPsZT9177Ao76A.jpeg)

![How to share a Process/Checklist with other users?](/images/kb/103000035185/IOjP2RfJP1nweO3jSSjmRSXSayCWBodyYw.jpeg)

Click the 3dots **⋮**  button as circled below to unfold the form Menu. 

Once unfolded, press **Share** and the share dialog of this form will appear as a window in the centre of your screen. The **participants** will be listed in the window if any are already set.

![How to share a Process/Checklist with other users?](/images/kb/103000035185/1i6zbeSMyir7lFdRyt5Y0CABLC6JyvRFxA.jpeg)

Enter the Team/User name to search and add as a **participant** or press the **X** to remove any existing **participants**.

When completed, simply press outside of the dialog window to save and finish.
