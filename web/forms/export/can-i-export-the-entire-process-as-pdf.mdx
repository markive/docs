## **Single download**

Click the 3 dots **⋮** icon on the upper-left hand side of your screen as circled below and select "**Download**" from the Menu options as shown below.

![Can I export the entire process as PDF?](/images/kb/103000035211/fFg9eBhGqSedmIKMSvsWjTfKYg1PPT3xtg.jpeg)

![Can I export the entire process as PDF?](/images/kb/103000035211/iqksrPHa6_qtYLMwWg56_-y4TDlflM1QEQ.jpeg)

Select your download preference from the resulting list here and click "**DOWNLOAD**"

![Can I export the entire process as PDF?](/images/kb/103000035211/61eRNWWQKOKLddmZYk-qKf9tSoWg_7OVFA.jpeg)

All done. Your process is downloaded!  
  
**What if there are numerous processes that have to be downloaded?** 

  
Not to worry, in **RDrive** you can download processes by batch. Let's go through how to do this below.

## **Batch Download**

After filtering the required process you need to export as PDF, choose "**SELECT**" by pressing the circled drop-down shown below.

![Can I export the entire process as PDF?](/images/kb/103000035211/ETNm2rCUn4j0_aLZri-bdfLMFfIbq3-34g.jpeg)

Once in "**SELECT**" mode, you will see there are two icons at the top right corner. These are shown below for clarity.

![Can I export the entire process as PDF?](/images/kb/103000035211/tAv6YTIr0KUFd5rgJwovAhBLQ5-HHL4a_w.jpeg)

![Can I export the entire process as PDF?](/images/kb/103000035211/g5MfiKrw8M3Utc7ATjsXM1EAsu8FyWldZQ.png)

Click the "**Select all**" icon and then click the "**Download**"  button next to it (circled below).

![Can I export the entire process as PDF?](/images/kb/103000035211/U2MqKxPr0Ws17EAzyJ8aNp0RwS_-pSWung.jpeg)

![Can I export the entire process as PDF?](/images/kb/103000035211/bNfNcprQOcsV8cDL-bLkR-hzfLo8emXjFw.jpeg)

Select your download preference from the resulting list and press "**DOWNLOAD**". Your batch download is complete!
