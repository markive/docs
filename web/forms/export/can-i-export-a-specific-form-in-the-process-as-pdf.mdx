![Can I export a specific form in the process as PDF?](/images/kb/103000035210/S6g4oyY3hFKYPSIv_KnAI9zBzVX-zooBjQ.png)

Once you are happy with your selections, click "**Download**"

## **FAQs & Troubleshooting**

**Q: Can I export a single process form as a PDF?**

A: Yes. Open the required process form and select the PDF or Export option to generate a downloadable PDF version of that specific form.

**Q: Why can’t I see the export PDF option?**  
**A:** The export option may be restricted by your project permissions or disabled within your organisation’s settings. Contact your project administrator if required.

**Q: My exported PDF is blank or incomplete—what should I do?**  
**A:** Ensure the form has been fully synced and saved before exporting. Refresh the page or reopen the form, then try the export again.
