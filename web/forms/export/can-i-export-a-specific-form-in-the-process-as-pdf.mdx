Instead of downloading the entire process you may just need to download a single form from a process.  
  
**RDrive** actually allows you to download forms individually, here are the steps to guide you through this below.

![Can I export a specific form in the process as PDF?](/images/kb/103000035210/QwIGdZtucipWilQ6cQ3yf2w3UmNsClyJoQ.jpeg)

Select the form you would like to download from the tabs and click the "**Edit**" pencil icon via the Menu options on the left-hand side of your page as shown below.

![Can I export a specific form in the process as PDF?](/images/kb/103000035210/nhHFL-2iWQTUDJ9uKRp8YVG4jfTYf0kbWA.png)

![Can I export a specific form in the process as PDF?](/images/kb/103000035210/hJpgFH5Zk2BlSPpjCZUlRCktiPj3WvmgHQ.png)

Once into the form, press the 3 dots **⋮** icon as shown below and select "**Download**" from the unfolded Menu options.

![Can I export a specific form in the process as PDF?](/images/kb/103000035210/goasxsY7K7vS-6b7daidWNdIrZ11nkdQPQ.jpeg)

![Can I export a specific form in the process as PDF?](/images/kb/103000035210/C9oLcUyBJCDrVrM1K6cvzqeGuEDdhavGtA.jpeg)

Select your download preference from the download form options list here and click "**DOWNLOAD**"

![Can I export a specific form in the process as PDF?](/images/kb/103000035210/dGQNz9TZmuJhFOvfdTSJj18T4_-EmhDCzA.jpeg)

All done! The PDF is downloaded!
