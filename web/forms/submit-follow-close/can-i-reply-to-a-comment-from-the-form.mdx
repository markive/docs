Once you got the email notification, you may want to reply to that [comment](https://snagr-support.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions/articles/103000035214-making-use-of-the-comment-function-) for the update.

* * *

Here is what you can do:

Click the **View item** button from the email notification to bring you the form viewer, or you may access the form viewer from the **finder** or **plan viewer**.

![Can I reply to a comment from the form? ?](/images/kb/103000035178/5yij7bLmrOpnTc8A1LbY2nK96WcNsQIFuA.png)

Click the **⋮** and select **COMMENT**.

![Can I reply to a comment from the form? ?](/images/kb/103000035178/x8gT6lAmZvg1UTnPw6h20ZdY9gMDmUWDLQ.jpeg)

![Can I reply to a comment from the form? ?](/images/kb/103000035178/a68t_PNG2jv1_-liB7V23w6H6kgGdYjtAQ.jpeg)

Click the "**Reply"** icon, enter your message, and click **REPLY** to save and send.

![Can I reply to a comment from the form? ?](/images/kb/103000035178/tQ1G9HDAu-rpKXHjJtfF6BBdpmP6ddDwZQ.jpeg)

![Can I reply to a comment from the form? ?](/images/kb/103000035178/thkaRH3u_4TSyBoYWEwGNvJ_WR-ejFQJcw.jpeg)

### **_Can I edit the comment?_**

You can only edit the comment in the first **5 mins**!  
After 5 mins, the comment is not allowed to edit.

To know how? Check below!
