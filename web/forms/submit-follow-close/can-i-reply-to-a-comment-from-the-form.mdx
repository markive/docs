### **Overview**

_A brief introduction explaining the purpose of this guide._

-   **Purpose:** How to respond to a comment on a form in RDrive on the website/browser
-   **Who It’s For:** General users

<Note>
Prerequisites: RDrive Access, Able to view processes
</Note>

  

Once you got the email notification, you may want to reply to that [comment](https://docs.rdrive.io/web/forms/submit-follow-close/making-use-of-the-comment-function) for the update, the comment will also show at the top of the email notification.

  

Click the **"View Form "**button from the email notification to bring you the form viewer, or you may access the form viewer from the **finder** or **plan viewer**.

![Can I reply to a comment on the form?](/images/kb/103000035178/jmoORQbPH7Drz9Bvl-tC5wlngIwWbpsu2w.png)

Click the **⋮** 3 dot menu in the top right.

  

![Can I reply to a comment on the form?](/images/kb/103000035178/XbOehbU7rsJ93Fu7VrEgGOBj9dkUJrHsyw.png)

  

Then go to **"View"** and select "**Comments"**.

  

![Can I reply to a comment on the form?](/images/kb/103000035178/pViwyibNLoNY42fuQPJBsKqjmPP58dDZyA.png) 

Click the "**Reply"** arrow icon, enter your message.

  

![Can I reply to a comment on the form?](/images/kb/103000035178/8NCHQlXc2dOGyr3WR57_HgxFfFGeQxYz6Q.png)

  

Click "**Comment"** to save and send your message.

  

![Can I reply to a comment on the form?](/images/kb/103000035178/HHxh7Itpa_sOckHMgfZjORVWwK-j8HFWmQ.png)

###   

### **Can I edit the comment?** 

**Please note: You can only edit the comment in the first 5 mins! After 5 mins, the comment will lock and wont be able to be edited.**

  

Click the 3 dot menu next to your comment.

![Can I reply to a comment on the form?](/images/kb/103000035178/47CBNaZ1D6jT4ysfHN1veXJaVjGY4YS5hg.png)

  

The select "**Edit**" to make changes to your comment.
