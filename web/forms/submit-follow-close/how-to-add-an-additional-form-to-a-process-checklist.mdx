Here are some scenarios where this function is needed:

1\. RISC – (Re-submission after inspection result is marked as failed)

2\. Site Diary – (Add an extra page to detail site activities and count for same day record)

When we add forms in the following way, **the form ID remains the same**

First, confirm the process/checklist form that needs an additional form in the sequence and click the 3 dots ⋮ button circled below to unfold the process/checklist/form. When unfolded, select "**Add Form**" as shown below.

![How to add an Additional Form to a Process/Checklist?](/images/kb/103000035209/SPT5FflAr5my-ooRTNsij4NnpvciG2IA8g.jpeg)

![How to add an Additional Form to a Process/Checklist?](/images/kb/103000035209/loVgwBKZIBMofcXBe8UT3knpYc8dFT0MSw.jpeg)

The add form window will open in the centre of your screen. Now you can select the form template to add in the sequence and press "**NEXT**". For the purposes of this example, we have chosen **RISC**.

![How to add an Additional Form to a Process/Checklist?](/images/kb/103000035209/gJIN25nANWUOorm7Df7V6iUtU23OnohTig.jpeg)

You can now drag and drop the form to decide where to place this in the sequence. Confirm by pressing "**OK**".

![How to add an Additional Form to a Process/Checklist?](/images/kb/103000035209/v5-_oUGLAeorekjOW4I3vgxHrziHvsrsMQ.jpeg)

When the new form is added into the sequence it will be viewable to you as circled below. 

Simply click into the **second form**, or go to the **second tab** to start editing the new extra form added into the sequence.

![How to add an Additional Form to a Process/Checklist?](/images/kb/103000035209/BE60Bv12f2Idr_Da8HfEGISuQZkRk8KjpA.jpeg)

**Why can’t I add an Additional Form?**

If you do not have the permission to add optional form, you won’t be able to do so. If you think you should have this ability, please reach out to your project administrator for more details.
