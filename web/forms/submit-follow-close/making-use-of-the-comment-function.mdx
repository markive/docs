### **Overview**

_How to add a comment to a process_

-   **Purpose:** Adding a comment and tagging a user in a process
-   **Who It’s For:** General users

<Note>
Prerequisites: Able to read the item
</Note>

  

On **RDrive**, there is a **comment** function for you to leave a comment on any given process/checklist form for your co-workers. 

  

An App/Email notification will also be sent to a user.

  

Here's how to add a comment to a process/checklist form, firstly, you will need to have your form open in the form viewer. Once in the form viewer, simply press the 3 dots icon highlighted below:

  

![Making use of the "Comment" Function?](/images/kb/103000035214/lbTmCTCFEGtXKd202iyFCoRHvVbR0JWz0w.png)

  

 Then select comments via the view option in the Menu dropdown as shown below.

  

![Making use of the "Comment" Function?](/images/kb/103000035214/9kEmDOt-_OLVhMwsYFHbPTqrDzBB-zcJsw.png)

  

Your options are as follows:  
  
Leave a general comment by typing in the text window.

  
If you would like to leave a comment to someone in particular, prompting a notification email, press the "**Add User**" icon highlighted below. When the User(s) have been added, press "**OK**" to confirm.

  

![Making use of the "Comment" Function?](/images/kb/103000035214/gyWg5XspN1OkD9bzcH0nFMDaLubEVyMSAw.png)

  

Select the user from the dropdown and then select "**OK**".

  

![Making use of the "Comment" Function?](/images/kb/103000035214/Ww-ERBGVT15cbKvblXb7oumYtju07r_URA.png)

  

When you finish entering your comment, press "**COMMENT**" to save.

  

![Making use of the "Comment" Function?](/images/kb/103000035214/vGPCX6dhWqaZWx7iOUlJT3YCuPaZO2ZKLA.png)

  

Any user(s) you selected will receive an email/app notification of your comment as shown below. Also, other users not added will also be able to [reply](https://intercom.help/rdrive/en/articles/4250584-can-i-reply-to-a-comment-from-the-form) to your comment. An example of the notification received is below.

![Making use of the "Comment" Function?](/images/kb/103000035214/sA6arJc3vwqj6JgYT3JheqdGfVmI67MZcQ.png)
