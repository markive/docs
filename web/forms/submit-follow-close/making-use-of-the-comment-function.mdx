**Have you thought about using RDrive as a communication channel?** 

  
On **RDrive**, there is a **comment** function for you to leave a comment on any given process/checklist form for your co-workers. 

An App/Email notification will also be sent to a user if you @mention them in the comment!

Here's how to add a comment to a process/checklist form.

Firstly, you will need to have your form open in the form viewer. Once in the form viewer, simply press the 3 dots icon and select comments via the view option in the Menu dropdown as shown below.

![Making use of the "Comment" Function?](/images/kb/103000035214/op-KvUS8ILUjf9ZqbhilQu0D3T9C2m_4MQ.jpeg)

![Making use of the "Comment" Function?](/images/kb/103000035214/My___0-SnC2CzEygwjC0qYxkdIS0OrYUYw.png)

Your options are as follows:  
  
Leave a general comment by typing in the text window.

  
If you would like to leave a comment to someone in particular, prompting a notification email, press the "**Add User**" icon circled below. When the User(s) have been added, press "**OK**" to confirm.

![Making use of the "Comment" Function?](/images/kb/103000035214/rJqhhzpg7mrIQOzNvjaVKDqNS3uASX-PgA.jpeg)![Making use of the "Comment" Function?](/images/kb/103000035214/NLhWxwlEijRMecD7aD8rL42KDpHEN-qiIw.jpeg)

When you finish entering your comment, press "**COMMENT**" to save.

![Making use of the "Comment" Function?](/images/kb/103000035214/DJkbw_1p8XITp_eLL5AMv8FQqCjYbMQyzw.jpeg)

Any user(s) you selected will receive an email/app notification of your comment as shown below. Also, other users not added will also be able to [reply](https://intercom.help/rdrive/en/articles/4250584-can-i-reply-to-a-comment-from-the-form) to your comment. An example of the notification received is below.

![Making use of the "Comment" Function?](/images/kb/103000035214/fd_v2WgP80QIaigpStBHCT6wQXXH6fmnTA.png)
