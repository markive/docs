### **Overview**

_How to edit a form within RDrive on the website/browser_

-   **Purpose:** How to edit and a form to add information
-   **Who It’s For:** General users

<Note>
Prerequisites: RDrive Access, able to edit the form
</Note>

  

* * *

**From the side menu / Category  
**  
In order to edit/update a form you will firstly need to select the correct form via the category and subcategory, Click the three line (hamburger menu) in the top right, we will select "**Defects"** then "**Inspection Reque****st**" for this example.  
  

Find the form you wish to edit and then click to select this, simply press the 3 dots ⋮ button as highlighted below and press "**Edit**" to open the form. 

  

![How to Edit/Update a Form?](/images/kb/103000035216/oOADUNGTTljnwQsFDJbeqxfRuAkOKPMmew.png)

  

In the form viewer, you should see the fields available for edit at the current status. 

  

In this example, the form is in **Draft** status (this can be seen towards the top of your screen in orange).

  

**Fields in red are mandatory fields, meaning you should fill out the fields before passing the form to the next status.**

  

![How to Edit/Update a Form?](/images/kb/103000035216/3HajWlS9yZhcvD5k7-bm7UxE-9cvBse6Rg.png)

  

Please note that any edits performed are **auto-saved** as standard. This means you are not required to click any Save button for your work to be stored.

  

However, to indicate the form is ready for the next party to review, you **must** **change the status** of the form. Failure to change the status of the form will result in the form workflow stagnating and not being visible to the next team along the workflow.

  

To change the status, simply press on the current status as circled below.

  

  

![How to Edit/Update a Form?](/images/kb/103000035216/J9HOjO30NG1lNxClbdPoxmsE7dcPnR2n5g.png)

  

Here you will see the form status window enlarged in the centre of your screen. 

In this example this form is newly created so there is only one history in record. Press "**CHANGE**" to update the status.

  

![How to Edit/Update a Form?](/images/kb/103000035216/fLvZEBAZqyUTxSwIekhA9sxMfxkrSk-l1w.png)

  

**In the screenshot below, we are showing all statuses of this form as a demonstration only.** **Please note that the terminology used on your project may also differ to those listed in the image below.**

  

In actual operation however, you will only see limited options based on overall **permission** settings. Please speak with your Project Admin team if you are unsure about your permissions.

## **FAQs & Troubleshooting**

  

**Q: Do I need to click save after editing a form?**  
A: No, changes are automatically saved as you edit.

**Q: Why can’t I see certain status options?**  
A: Status options depend on your role permissions. Contact your **Project Admin** for access clarification.

**Q: What happens if I forget to update the form’s status?**  
A: The form will remain in its current stage and will not move forward in the workflow.
