### **Overview**

_How to change the location of a process within RDrive using the desktop/website_

-   **Purpose:** Changing the location of processes within RDrive
-   **Who It’s For:** Manager of controlling team

<Note>
Prerequisites: RDrive Access, control rights to move the process.
</Note>

###   
**Mov****ing a Process in RDrive**

  

You can change the location of a process in RDrive. Here’s how to do it:

  

-   [](#Via-Plan-Viewer)[Via Plan Viewer](#Via-Plan-Viewer)
-   [Via Finder](#Via-Finder)

  

_**Important:**_ _Not everyone has the permission to move a process from one location to another. If you're unable to perform this action, please contact your project administrator for assistance._

* * *

####   

####   

#### **Via Plan Viewer**

1.   Click the Location/sticky note icon to display the process on the left side of your screen.![Can I change the location of a Process?](/images/kb/103000035196/mXx6D9RNs32DH-vew6nhEYdQzuF4AJlG8A.png)  
    
      
      
    
2.  Click the three dots (**⋮**) to open the dropdown menu, then select "**Move**."![Can I change the location of a Process?](/images/kb/103000035196/QuhwZlilgGrr4K1o0s3UKxYh-GZXrclExA.png)
    
3.  Choose the new location for the process and click "**OK**" to confirm.![Can I change the location of a Process?](/images/kb/103000035196/iucjA5OPzBrionbC-KzRQl8kMH5pQJ4dNQ.png)_  
    Your process has been successfully moved to another location on the same drawing!_
    

####   

####   

#### **_How about moving a Process to a Location on Another Drawing?_**

RDrive allows you to relocate processes to different drawings as well.

1.  Start by clicking the "**Move**" icon again.![Can I change the location of a Process?](/images/kb/103000035196/_5ORST9ecivPcQl1EbTiFMmnlu0Hq1mnJw.png)
    
2.  Use the unified search bar to find the desired drawing by typing "Drawing" and selecting the relevant option from the list.  
      
    ![Can I change the location of a Process?](/images/kb/103000035196/JMc08WRohMGGmeaGJjBQ3noGDGtGs2oFGQ.png)  
    The chosen drawing will appear on your screen. Select the correct location and click "**OK**" to confirm.![Can I change the location of a Process?](/images/kb/103000035196/rmpm7Yv4p2U6qnBG9fg8t3bCZuvnUh6Asg.png)_Your process has now been moved to another location on a different drawing!_

## **Via Finder**

1.  To locate the relevant process, apply filters using the unified search bar at the top of your screen.![Can I change the location of a Process?](/images/kb/103000035196/UTfKI91mvUfXmz0eA_4XIfQE1nVtIaE1GQ.png)
    
2.  Once you find the process, click the three dots (**⋮**) to open the dropdown menu.![Can I change the location of a Process?](/images/kb/103000035196/xPEe9NaBk5RADAabKQRRYwVz1NqItfbYBA.png)
    
3.  Click "**Move**" to reveal the options for changing the process's location.![Can I change the location of a Process?](/images/kb/103000035196/HdIMkZhfgJjD-QpIJNYeXll5teoBQ-PG9g.png)
    
4.  In the expanded menu, select the new location.  
      
    **_Note:_** _If you want to move the process to a location on a different drawing, make sure to change both the drawing and location options before clicking "OK" to confirm._  
    ![Can I change the location of a Process?](/images/kb/103000035196/9F1a6n6Y-kdGiW-UKWhHJpz56W-oMPxF0w.png)
    
    _Your process has now been successfully moved!_

    FAQs & Troubleshooting
    
    Q: What if I cant see the "Move" function in the three dot menu?  
    A: This is likely down to you not having permission in the team you are working in, change team or reach out to your project administrator.  
    Q: Why hasn't the location stamped in the form changed?  
    A: This is stamped with the originating location for audit purposes.
