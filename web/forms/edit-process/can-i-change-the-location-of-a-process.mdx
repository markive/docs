### **Mov****ing a Process in RDrive**

You can change the location of a process in RDrive. Here’s how to do it:

-   [](#via-plan-viewer)[Via Plan Viewer](#via-plan-viewer)
-   [Via Finder](#via-finder)

_**Important:**_ _Not everyone has the permission to move a process from one location to another. If you're unable to perform this action, please contact your project administrator for assistance._

* * *

#### **Via Plan Viewer**

1.  If the Plan Viewer is congested, use filters in the unified search bar at the top of your screen to find the process you need.![Can I change the location of a Process?](/images/kb/103000035196/8I0QFwpSPKGaZ5ENJ_5MxR0N9WvGnHWHAg.jpeg)
    
2.  Once you’ve identified the correct location, click the memo/post-it icon to display the process details on the left side of your screen.![Can I change the location of a Process?](/images/kb/103000035196/7f6wfrLYtQwYwTg8-DvYvh6pW36_ykco7A.jpeg)
    
3.  Click the three dots (**⋮**) to open the dropdown menu, then select "**Move**."![Can I change the location of a Process?](/images/kb/103000035196/gZazWwL4K9cLEthGX8aRW4HJGhh2kk5V8g.jpeg)
    
4.  Choose the new location for the process and click "**OK**" to confirm.![Can I change the location of a Process?](/images/kb/103000035196/n1LLi0w9aqFD_y8V5wySovxm3qmwJZOF5w.jpeg)_  
    ![Can I change the location of a Process?](/images/kb/103000035196/f219dNs4yiESuGyPboikP7nBrkFV-d7yKA.png)Your process has been successfully moved to another location on the same drawing!_
    

#### **_How about moving a Process to a Location on Another Drawing?_**

RDrive allows you to relocate processes to different drawings as well.

1.  Start by clicking the "**Move**" icon again.  
    ![Can I change the location of a Process?](/images/kb/103000035196/rdmdFvRWLZYuYKYblMVR_TJLnLEFxmjfqA.png)
    
2.  Use the unified search bar to find the desired drawing by typing "Drawing" and selecting the relevant option from the list.  
    The chosen drawing will appear on your screen. Select the correct location and click "**OK**" to confirm.![Can I change the location of a Process?](/images/kb/103000035196/qmAAN4Kyyk6yWr_S2qasbeRhAWeYHNgknw.jpeg)_Your process has now been moved to another location on a different drawing!_
    

* * *

## **Via Finder**

1.  To locate the relevant process, apply filters using the unified search bar at the top of your screen.![Can I change the location of a Process?](/images/kb/103000035196/Hns4qPNIPinTQL4Ff0-c2MKt58JC-fNimA.jpeg)
    
2.  Once you find the process, click the three dots (**⋮**) to open the dropdown menu.![Can I change the location of a Process?](/images/kb/103000035196/nT_zy76QQcXluVvKjoubRL0zk6YUvUJlxA.jpeg)
    
3.  Click "**Move**" to reveal the options for changing the process's location.![Can I change the location of a Process?](/images/kb/103000035196/SSbXepRcMb9BPDrHX9BCQbjRTq1iOx6Ykw.jpeg)
    
4.  In the expanded menu, select the new location.  
      
    **_Note:_** _If you want to move the process to a location on a different drawing, make sure to change both the drawing and location options before clicking "OK" to confirm._  
    ![Can I change the location of a Process?](/images/kb/103000035196/B7nUi3wiJCarF_md6W3UyBHp15D_j5h-oQ.jpeg)  
    
    _Your process has now been successfully moved!_
    
    ![Can I change the location of a Process?](/images/kb/103000035196/QFTk7ZJeUwv2sDME_rKeaTDxRwNJg7as-Q.png)
