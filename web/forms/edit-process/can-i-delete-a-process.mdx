### **Overview**

_How to delete a process within RDrive_

-   **Purpose:** How to permanently delete a process within RDrive.
-   **Who It’s For:** Admins, project teams

<Note>
Prerequisites: Able to delete the process
</Note>

  

**RDrive** allows you to delete a process, let's go through how this can be done.

  

**The reason for this is that** **deleting anything on RDrive is IRREVERSIBLE. You are NOT able to reverse the deleted action.**

  

  

  

**via Plan Viewer**

  

Search the process by applying filters in unified search bar located at the top of your screen. 

  

![Can I Delete a Process?](/images/kb/103000035183/9uew2PL8RMAnuvjLdp04E1low-UDIcSYpQ.png)

  

Once you have the required location, simply click into the memo/post it icon to unfold the process details on the left-hand side of your screen as shown below.

  

![Can I Delete a Process?](/images/kb/103000035183/-0_SGwemmHTjsYGykp8GtDskDTUUn96Xhw.png)

  

Select the relevant process from the options on the left-hand side of your screen as shown below.

  

![Can I Delete a Process?](/images/kb/103000035183/_fKk9dlCwl6P40y-OUQVy6YZlatCZJzQtA.png)

  

Now press the 3 dots **⋮** icon.

  

![Can I Delete a Process?](/images/kb/103000035183/03CA2uMNvr6GvKQFc0i5zs3chrIgFA46IA.png)

  

This will expand the dropdown menu and select "**Delete**".

  

![Can I Delete a Process?](/images/kb/103000035183/aoiLMMAM2z0boyGmE3NfCHzHC0NQ0beBjQ.png)

  

You must then click "**DELETE**" again to confirm you are deleting the process.

  

![Can I Delete a Process?](/images/kb/103000035183/eyl3OJ0qyUE8bX6kzPtMS5zeHl5en_tHvQ.png)

# **via Process Finder**

From the process finder, use the universal search bar to find the item.

  

![Can I Delete a Process?](/images/kb/103000035183/0SOoo_0--GAc_-vcG5eRazrZLLqwaLMlnA.png)

  

Once you have the item selected, click the 3 dot menu on the item to open the dropdown.

  

![Can I Delete a Process?](/images/kb/103000035183/1mX0ZUy4pFbrjGysdbW8RsCZ3_jhAIk3ng.png)

  

From the dropdown menu and select "**Delete**" as shown below.

  

![Can I Delete a Process?](/images/kb/103000035183/TH91XsNjgz5YR6KtzGt-X1J84kGYQ-8BYg.png)

  

You must then click "**DELETE**" again to confirm you are deleting the process.

  

![Can I Delete a Process?](/images/kb/103000035183/dZN623Q0SUKyiyOoNejk0ABr0Ps00tfMEA.png)
