**RDrive** allows you to delete a process, let's go through how this can be done.  
  
**We recommend only Project Administrators have the "Delete" right and they should only perform it when it is absolutely necessary.** 

**The reason for this is that** **deleting anything on RDrive is IRREVERSIBLE. You are NOT able to reverse the deleted action.**

**via Plan Viewer**

Search the process by applying filters in unified search bar located at the top of your screen. 

![Can I Delete a Process?](/images/kb/103000035183/IsLN3E_yMgZkXLYqv31W7kMi7IuB9yNbpw.jpeg)

Once you have the required location, simply click into the memo/post it icon to unfold the process details on the left-hand side of your screen as shown below.

![Can I Delete a Process?](/images/kb/103000035183/qRj7sQ0szntIea-am2STDMNl4tL3F7v8EQ.jpeg)

Select the relevant process from the options on the left-hand side of your screen as shown below.

![Can I Delete a Process?](/images/kb/103000035183/u3eKf0QbTdls-3sE0oem_hLc0E8LpaVQbQ.jpeg)

Now press the 3 dots **⋮** icon to explore the dropdown menu and select "**Delete**". 

You must then click "**DELETE**" again to confirm you are deleting the process.

![Can I Delete a Process?](/images/kb/103000035183/dthNBD3_suIoIx-pAuioLVIrIKCQM9rNCg.jpeg)

![Can I Delete a Process?](/images/kb/103000035183/AGb_1ynvL34tv_wRdFQ5H8AWCamyaFItjw.jpeg)

# **via Process Finder**

First of all, search the process you would like to delete through unified search bar at the top of your screen.

Once selected, press the 3 dots **⋮** icon to explore the dropdown menu and select "**Delete**" as shown below. 

You must then click "**DELETE**" again to confirm you are deleting the process.
