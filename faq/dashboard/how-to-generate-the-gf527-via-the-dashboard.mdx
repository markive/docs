## **How to Use RDrive's Dashboard to Generate the GF527 Report for DWSS Compliance**

RDrive has introduced a dedicated dashboard to generate the GF527 report, which is essential for complying with the **Digital Works Supervision System (DWSS)** requirements. This guide will walk you through the steps to effectively use this feature, ensuring your project data aligns with the standards expected for DWSS reporting.

-   [Step 1: Access the Dashboard](#step-1%3a-access-the-dashboard)
-   [Step 2: Select Report Options](#step-2%3a-select-report-options)
-   [Step 3: Trigger Report Generation](#step-3%3a-trigger-report-generation)
-   [Step 4: Wait for the Email](#step-4%3a-wait-for-the-email)
-   [Step 5: Download the GF527 Report](#step-5%3a-download-the-gf527-report)

* * *

### **What is GF527?**

The **GF527** is a form used in the construction industry in Hong Kong to report work supervision data. Compliance with this form is a requirement for projects using DWSS, as it ensures accurate and consistent recording of on-site activities. RDrive simplifies this process by providing a built-in dashboard to generate the GF527 report directly.

* * *

## **Step-by-Step Guide to Generate the GF527 Report**

### **Step 1: Access the Dashboard**

-   Log in to your RDrive account.
-   Navigate to the Dashboard, select our **GF527** / **Labour Return** Dashboard. This is where you can manage and generate your reports.![How to generate the GF527 via the Dashboard?](/images/kb/103000316800/e2uVf9yPMka741UIyggH0N-0BGPSDYP8Hg.png)

### **Step 2: Select Report Options**

-   Choose the **GF527 type**: Select between **2017** or **2003** depending on your project's requirement.
-   Select the **month** and **year** for which you want to generate the report. Note that you can only select **one month and one year** at a time.
-   Choose the **report type**:
    -   **PDF** for easy viewing and printing.
    -   **Excel** for data analysis and further modifications.
-   Enter the **email address** of the user who should receive the generated report.![How to generate the GF527 via the Dashboard?](/images/kb/103000316800/4TsoJdRWvvPPXq5D3fEvtHHjE-9DAoL7ug.png)

### **Step 3: Trigger Report Generation**

-   After making your selections, click the **CONFIRM AND SEND** button.![How to generate the GF527 via the Dashboard?](/images/kb/103000316800/foPXiCUoOxp1iwXLLnrDhJPhEzv0aiFW2g.png)  
      
    
-   The report generation process will start in the background, allowing you to **close this page** and continue other tasks on RDrive.![How to generate the GF527 via the Dashboard?](/images/kb/103000316800/W_MMOJCemjBi-OQP3Hk3r1EbgJ-lTlJJwQ.png)

### **Step 4: Wait for the Email**

-   Once the report is ready, RDrive will automatically send a notification email to the provided recipient.
-   The generation time may vary depending on the data size, so please be patient while the system processes the request.  
      
    

### **Step 5: Download the GF527 Report**

-   The **GF527 report** will be attached as a file in the email, either in **PDF** or **Excel** format as you selected.
-   Open the email and download the attached report—you now have everything you need to fulfill the DWSS requirements.![How to generate the GF527 via the Dashboard?](/images/kb/103000316800/exs9XCDye89PA8rBUUhfSTTKV9kWPVp4og.jpeg)

## _**Important Tip: Data Refresh on the Dashboard**_

_The data displayed on the RDrive dashboard is **not real-time**. It refreshes based on a schedule. You can view the last refresh time at the bottom right of the dashboard. Only data entered **before** this time will appear. For urgent updates, please contact **RDrive Support** for help._
