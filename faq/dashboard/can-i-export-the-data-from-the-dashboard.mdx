title: "Can I export the data from the Dashboard?"
description: "Click the menu icon ≡ on the top left corner to unfold the Menu, get entry to the Dashboard to see custom analytics/summary."
---

Yes, you can. :)

Click the menu icon **≡** on the top left corner to unfold the Menu, get entry to the **Dashboard** to see custom analytics/summary.

Custom analytics/summary are built with the data you gathered using RDrive.

At the drop down list, it displays multiple dashboards that you are given access to.

![Can I export the data from the Dashboard?](/images/kb/103000035028/Ow1NOwYYc4eKyt2-DWP7y_GcRpfued9HdA.jpeg)

To export, you may move your cursor to the table you need, click the ... at the top right corner, and select export data.
