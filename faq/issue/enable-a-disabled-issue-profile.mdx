### **via Website**

  
You will able to see the menu icon on the top left corner, please click it to unfold the menu, access the **Settings** > **Categories**, select the category and **subcategory**.

pick the disabled profile.

![Enable a disabled Issue profile](/images/kb/103000035118/ShCYyzWqCCqqU8yMhjCw0OzpAS007SJ9dg.jpeg)

Select **Enable** to reactivate profile.
