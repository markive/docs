The setup can be highly customized within each company. In most cases, custom issues are typically designed for defects, safety issues, environmental issues, or photo diaries. For each of these categories, it is possible they have their own icon to represent and differentiate themselves on the plan viewer.

Please check out the legend to learn what each icon represents.

* * *

### **Website -**

Right-click on the plan viewer, and select **Legend.**
