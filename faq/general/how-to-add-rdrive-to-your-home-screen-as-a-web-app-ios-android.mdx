### **Overview**

  
_This guide explains how to quickly add **RDrive** from your mobile browser (Safari or Chrome) to your device’s home screen. Adding RDrive as a Web App allows you to access it like a native app, without needing to download anything from the App Store or Google Play._  
  

-   **Purpose:** To guide users in saving RDrive as a Web App shortcut on their iOS or Android device.
-   **Who It’s For:** All users who prefer quick one-tap access to RDrive on their mobile devices.  
      
    

<Note>
Prerequisites: A stable internet connection, User login credentials
</Note>

* * *

## **Step-by-Step Instructions**  
  

### **✅ Step 1: Open RDrive in Your Browser**

-   On **iOS**: 
    1.  Open **Safari / **Chrome****.
    2.  Navigate to your project’s RDrive URL (e.g., `https://{yourproject}.rdrive.io`).
-   On **Android**: 
    1.  Open **Chrome**.
    2.  Navigate to your project’s RDrive URL (e.g., `https://{yourproject}.rdrive.io`).

### **✅ Step 2: Open the Share or Menu Options**

-   **iOS (Safari):**
    1.  Tap the **Share icon** (square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen.
        
-   **Android (Chrome):**
    1.  Tap the **three-dot menu (⋮)** at the top right corner of the screen.
        

### **✅ Step 3: Add to Home Screen**

-   **iOS (Safari):**
    1.  Scroll down the Share menu.
        
    2.  Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
        
-   **Android (Chrome):**
    1.  From the menu, select **Add to Home screen**.
        
    2.  Confirm the shortcut name (e.g., “RDrive”) and tap **Add**.
        

### **✅ Step 4: Confirm and Access the RDrive Web App**

1.  A new RDrive icon will now appear on your device’s home screen.
2.  Tap it to launch RDrive directly, just like a mobile app.
3.  Log in as usual to start using RDrive.

##   
**FAQs & Troubleshooting**

**Q: Do I need to install RDrive from the App Store or Google Play?**  
A: No. Adding RDrive as a Web App from the browser creates a shortcut on your home screen with app-like access.

**Q: The “Add to Home Screen” option doesn’t appear. What should I do?**  
A: Make sure you are using **Safari**, **Chrome** on iOS or **Chrome** on Android. Other browsers may not support this feature.

**Q: Will RDrive work offline after being added as a Web App?**  
A: No. An internet connection is required to use RDrive web app. Only RDrive APP can work offline.
