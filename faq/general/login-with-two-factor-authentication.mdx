## **Login to** **Website**

For log in to RDrive, the two authentication factors are:  
**1\. User credential** (Email address and account password)  
**2\. 1️⃣** **[Approved by the authenticated device.](#1%ef%b8%8f%e2%83%a3-approved-by-the-authenticated-device.%c2%a0)**   
or  
**Passcode generated by RDrive**. (2️⃣ [Send by email](#2%ef%b8%8f%e2%83%a3%c2%a0use-passcode---send-passcode-to-email) or [3️⃣ get from RDrive APP](#3%ef%b8%8f%e2%83%a3-use-passcode---from-rdrive-app))

Let's start.

Enter the **Email address, password**, and click **LOG IN.**

## 1️⃣ Approved by the authenticated device**.** 

  
Login requests will send to the **authenticated** device as a push-up notification.  
Please unlock the device and click Approve.

## _Hints and Tips_ 

## _Do I need to remember the 6-digit verification code?_

No, the 6-digit passcode **regenerates automatically every 30 seconds** from RDrive.
