A controller serves as the responsible team overseeing a particular item. Initially, control is assigned to the team that creates the item and can be transferred to different teams as needed.

To modify the controller, follow these steps:

## Changing Controller via Control Option:

-   [\- Process](#--process)
-   [\- Issue/record](#--issue/record)

### \- Process

1.  Find the Process from Plan viewer or Finder.
2.  Click the ⋮ button to reveal a menu.![What is Controller and how to change?](/images/kb/103000035073/kVig3FlPIZ7qpoNyzr5PTutkkoBwy7vYZQ.png)
3.  Select "Control" from the options available.![What is Controller and how to change?](/images/kb/103000035073/gBJwxHTC1MLmIi7tQyNL_thsX3tBj3tssg.png)

The current controller will be displayed.

By default, the team that created the item becomes the initial controller.

1.  Click on "CHANGE" to initiate the controller change.![What is Controller and how to change?](/images/kb/103000035073/ZDb5bodWVvRCBYhWLmSiEhKCK_fwo5s2dA.png)
2.  Choose the team you wish to assign control to, then confirm by selecting "OK."![What is Controller and how to change?](/images/kb/103000035073/HwLzYM33v44hqODpDxV11RQ8ygMoWAecRw.png)

It's worth noting that the controller can also be modified through [assignment rules.](https://docs.rdrive.io/setup/project/category-issue-profiles/assignment-rule-issue-profile) This process ensures the appropriate team is in control of the item.
