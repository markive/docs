### Stay inquisitive!

By clicking the button, you'll reveal a menu that unlocks a world of additional functionalities.

Take "**Privileges**," for instance.

Users can easily review the permissions granted for a specific action or record by tapping the ⋮ button and opting for "**Privileges**." All the actions on that page will be presented with clear ✅ (allowed) or ❌ (restricted) indicators.

This feature will be accessible across various sections like the finder, location on the plan viewer, form viewer, and more.

And, the administrators hold the power to assess this for all users. Simply click on a name to switch to user view and inspect the permissions.
