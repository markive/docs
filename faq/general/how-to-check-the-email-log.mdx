### **Email Log**

_This guide explains how to access and review the email log in the system._

-   **Purpose: This guide helps users track sent emails, including their details and statuses.**
-   **Who It’s For: General users, admins, or anyone needing to check email logs.  
      
    **

<Note>
Prerequisites: Proper access permissions to view the email log.
</Note>

* * *

## **Step-by-Step Instructions**

✅ **Step 1: Access the Email Log**  
Navigate to the side menu and go to **Logs > Email logs**.![How to Check the Email Log](/images/kb/103000342929/ycegtpo57k9BcZOUqKcADZ4tzbJTRohHbA.jpeg)  

✅ **Step 2: View Filtered Emails**  
The system will automatically filter emails sent from the previous day. You will see yesterday's date set in the **unified search bar.**

✅ **Step 3: Review the Displayed Emails**  
The email log page will display emails sorted by the **receiver (user)**.![How to Check the Email Log](/images/kb/103000342929/8GCJ11OUKjMsd99R03Gy7SuXGr98qXtX2g.jpeg)

✅ **Step 4: Check Email Details**  
Click on an email entry to expand its details. The following information will be available:

-   **Type**: Notification type
    
-   **Email Type**: Specifies the category of email
    
-   **Email Title**: Same as the email subject
    
-   **Status**: Indicates the item status (for status notification emails)
    
-   **Sent Date**: The date the email was sent
    
-   **Acknowledged Date**: The date the receiver clicked the link in the email
    

![How to Check the Email Log](/images/kb/103000342929/4-Xjh2EIIm_43-8qwhzoXedOpPnZHehgtw.png)  

Clicking into the email allows you to view more detailed information about the message.![How to Check the Email Log](/images/kb/103000342929/UCF_2xUy-v-vZFEybbeReN-YFE9HorwVZw.jpeg)

✅ **Step 5: Use Additional Search Criteria**  
The **unified search bar** allows filtering emails based on different criteria. Here are the available search options:![How to Check the Email Log](/images/kb/103000342929/qzxAZl0cGFUQbYKjH7wjXa44b9d2K_dpHA.jpeg)

## **FAQs & Troubleshooting**

Q: **Why don’t I see any emails?**  
A: Ensure the correct date filter is applied in the search bar.

Q: **Why can’t I see the acknowledgment date?**  
A: The acknowledgment date only appears if the receiver has clicked the link in the email.

Q: **How do I filter emails by a specific user?**  
A: Use the unified search bar to enter the user’s details and refine the results.
