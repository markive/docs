## Exploring the differences between the App and Web versions.

RDrive is the Field Digitalisation Platform. It’s the fastest way to capture, monitor & analyse your project data.

At RDrive, we adopt the most cutting-edge technologies and help you best utilise what technology offers. The real empowerment lies within the change in how people think and the way they approach problems again knowing the possibilities of technologies.

* * *

## RDrive offers two modes of usage: the Web version and the App. Let's delve into the differences between the two:

**Web Version:**

1.  Real-time updates: The Web version provides real-time updates, allowing all data to be immediately uploaded to the server, ensuring that your project data is always up-to-date  
      
    
2.  Access from any computer or device with a browser: The Web version can be accessed from any computer or device with a browser, making it convenient for managing projects across different devices.

**App Version:**

1.  Offline work: The App is designed for offline work, making it indispensable for scenarios where a stable internet connection may not be available.  
      
    
2.  [Syncing](https://snagr-support.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions/articles/103000035199) capability: Offline work necessitates the importance of syncing. Regular synchronization is required to upload and download project details, ensuring that your data stays in sync with the server.  
      
    
3.  Location data capture: When taking photos using the App, location data can be captured, providing accurate geolocation information for your project photos.  
      
    
4.  QR code and NFC scanning: The App allows for scanning QR codes and NFC to input data, and scan the location QR codes to direct for adding records.

* * *

## In conclusion:

Whether you choose the Web version or the App, RDrive remains a powerful digitalization platform, making it effortless for you to capture, monitor, and analyze project data. The decision between the two versions depends on your specific needs. If you require real-time updates and cross-device accessibility, the Web version might be more suitable. On the other hand, if you need offline capabilities and location data capture, the App will be your best choice. Regardless of your preference, RDrive will assist you in achieving digital transformation, enhancing project management efficiency, and accuracy.
