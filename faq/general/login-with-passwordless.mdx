## **Login to** **Website**

You need below 2 detail to log in RDrive:  
**1\. User credential** (Email address)  
**2\. 1️⃣** **[Approved by the authenticated device](#1%ef%b8%8f%e2%83%a3-approved-by-the-authenticated-device.%c2%a0).**   
or  
**Passcode generated by RDrive**. (2️⃣ [Send by email](#2%ef%b8%8f%e2%83%a3%c2%a0use-passcode---send-passcode-to-email) or 3️⃣ [get from RDrive APP](#3%ef%b8%8f%e2%83%a3-use-passcode---from-rdrive-app))

Enter the **Email address** and click **log in.**

![Login with Passwordless](/images/kb/103000035151/wv0xBElLk-alibrx4WEhsy55ZFlRnCZ5LA.jpeg)

## **1️⃣** **Approved by the authenticated device.** 

  
Login request will send to the authenticated device as a push-up notification.  
Please unlock the device and click **Approve.**

  
_[Authenticated device is means a device already logged in with 2FA. For the first log in, you have to authenticate by the PASSCODE from email.](#2%ef%b8%8f%e2%83%a3%c2%a0use-passcode---send-passcode-to-email-2)_

## _Hints and Tips_ 

### _Do I need to remember the 6-digit verification code?_

No, the 6-digit passcode **regenerates automatically every 30 seconds** from RDrive.

###   
_How can I reset the **authenticated device**?_ 

Click [here](https://snagr-support.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions/articles/103000035046-edit-my-account-settings) to know more.
