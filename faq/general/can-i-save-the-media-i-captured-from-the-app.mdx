### **Overview**

_How to save media taken within RDrive to your device_

-   **Purpose:** To make an additional copy of media taken within RDrive to your device
-   **Who It’s For:**\[General users

<Note>
Prerequisites: RDrive Access, available storage on the device
</Note>

  

You may wish to save images taken within within the RDrive application.  
  

  
Here's how to enable it:

  

Swipe up to display more options and click on the "**Settings**" button within the "**Shortcuts and tools**" section.![Can I save the media I captured from the APP?](/images/kb/103000164048/tcCETGec4pXqA_rmUBuzWWGykob1LQnnoA.png)

  

Select "**Settings**" from the pull up menu.

  

![Can I save the media I captured from the APP?](/images/kb/103000164048/ejR0YDhY9gNV5gTYcRw6LA_i9JrvfjWskQ.png)

  

From the "**Camera**" section, enable the option that suits your needs—whether it's capturing images or videos.   
  
Remember to click **Save** to confirm in the top right to confirm and save your preferences.

  

![Can I save the media I captured from the APP?](/images/kb/103000164048/jT2hKCFD9kahiI9L4-PZg0kZteDr42hmyw.png)

* * *

Once you have enabled the preferred option, every photo or video you capture using our APP will be securely saved in your local storage. 

  

  

### **FAQs & Troubleshooting**

  

**Q: Why can't I save or download media from the app?**

**A**: Your user permissions may restrict downloading media, or your device may not have sufficient storage available. Check your permissions and device settings before trying again.

**Q: The media I captured is not appearing in the project—what should I do?**

**A:** Ensure your device has successfully synced with the server. If you were working offline, connect to the internet and perform a sync to upload the media.
