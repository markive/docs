### **Overview**

_This guide explains how users can receive notifications in RDrive based on subscriptions, and automation rules._

  

-   **Purpose:** To help users understand how to receive updates about items they’re involved with.
-   **Who It’s For:** General users, project admins.

  

<Note>
Prerequisites: Access to items in RDrive and appropriate permissions.
</Note>

## **Situation-by-Situation Instructions**

### ✅ **Situation 1: I want to get notified about updates on an Item I'm involved in**

-   Navigate to the desired item.
    
-   Click the dropdown menu.
    
-   Select **Subscribe** to start receiving notifications for status changes and updates.  
      
    

### ✅ **Situation 2: I want to subscribe other teammates to an Item**

-   If you have the required permissions:
    
    -   Open the item.
        
    -   Click the dropdown menu > **Subscribe**.
        
    -   Add or remove users in the dialog.
        
    -   Confirm to save your changes.  
          
        

### ✅ **Situation 3: I want notifications to be triggered by automation**

-   Project administrators can configure **automation rules** that automatically subscribe or unsubscribe users based on the item's status.
    
-   Example scenarios:
    
    -   Auto-subscribe the responsible party when an item changes to "In Progress".
        
    -   Unsubscribe users when the item is marked as "Closed".
        
-   ✉️ Contact your **project admin** if you'd like to implement or adjust automation rules.  
      
    

### ✅ **Situation 4: I want to receive mobile push notifications**

-   To receive push notifications on the RDrive mobile app:
    
    -   Make sure the **notification permission** is enabled in your device settings.
        
    -   Ensure you are logged into the correct **project or company**.
        
    -   Confirm that you are **subscribed** to the item.

## **FAQs & Troubleshooting**

❓ **I’m not receiving notifications even though I subscribed.**  
✔️ **APP** - Check that your device has notifications enabled and that you’re logged into the correct project.  
✔️ **For email** - Please check your email’s junk or spam folder, or contact your company’s IT team to ensure the sender’s domain is approved and not blocked.

  

❓ **Can I receive notifications without being the assignee?**  
✔️ Yes. As long as you’re subscribed to the item, you’ll receive relevant notifications.

  

❓ **What’s the difference between being subscribed and being assigned?**  
✔️ Subscribed users receive notifications but are not responsible for the item. Assigned users are responsible.
