---

The width of the drop down menu is tied to the width of the field, so its text can sometimes be truncated when the field is too small.

![How to view the text in full in form viewer drop down menu when it is truncated?](/images/kb/103000035098/o7VLg67PUtpwMlFnvtTPSUREgmISGA62GA.png)

You can zoom in on the form to give it more space to display the content in full.

![How to view the text in full in form viewer drop down menu when it is truncated?](/images/kb/103000035098/dd7Xt2QWOMuzWKrDDx5dnwZQtX0i3ME-2Q.png)
