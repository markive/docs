Account settings refer to the customizable options and configurations associated with a user's

account within a system, application, or online platform. These settings allow users to personalize and manage various aspects of their accounts to suit their preferences, security requirements, and

usage needs. 

* * *

To modify your account preferences, first, log in to the system and navigate to the top right corner

where your profile icon is located.

![Edit my Account Settings](/images/kb/103000035046/RriSITFBHe-5tpFflNalCIqw7DjK2ibs4w.png)

Click on the profile icon and choose "**Account Settings**". Within this section, you'll find four categories of account information available for customization:

-   [Profile](#profile)
-   [Password](#password)
-   [Devices](#devices)
-   [Credentials](#credentials)

![Edit my Account Settings](/images/kb/103000035046/vDg3MHhjUwElXHN23UyvO_8rF6jJmy_ITg.png)

* * *

## Profile

In this segment, you can view and adjust your display name, registered email address, and current language.  
Please note that only the system language is editable. If changes are needed for the display name and email address, kindly contact the project administrator for assistance.  
  
Click the button provided to initiate language modifications.

![Edit my Account Settings](/images/kb/103000035046/KAYDhGnHGOQOUGA9hLHwMF0Amz6_xsOjFg.jpeg)

Select to change.

![Edit my Account Settings](/images/kb/103000035046/hNWgTPJazGnTiEjIgf5nFrrxfIylaV0zzA.jpeg)

* * *

## Password

## To reset your password, input the current password, then provide the new password twice. Confirm the changes by selecting **OK**.

![Edit my Account Settings](/images/kb/103000035046/Bv-kZel82FOCs8nj77mAfh-rtHQfBfmYdQ.jpeg)

## Devices

If 2FA (Two-Factor Authentication) or Passwordless authentication is active for your project, managing authenticated devices is crucial. Review and remove devices as needed.
