### **Overview**

_How to access and edit your account settings from the website/desktop_

-   **Purpose:** How to navigate to account settings and add preference
-   **Who It’s For:** General users

<Note>
Prerequisites: RDrive Access
</Note>

  

  

  

  

Account settings refer to the customizable options and configurations associated with a user's account within a system, application, or online platform. These settings allow users to personalize and manage various aspects of their accounts to suit their preferences, security requirements, and usage needs. 

  

To modify your account preferences, first, log in to the system and navigate to the top right corner where your profile icon is located.

![Edit my Account Settings](/images/kb/103000035046/UDk5GDn5ml4UgpQi_UtYRMupUpMoW-HLpQ.png)

  

Click on the profile icon and choose "**Account Settings**". Within this section, you'll find four categories of account information available for customization:

![Edit my Account Settings](/images/kb/103000035046/qj9juHbDj-8YjQ1wqRncluqF-aSr9RgJzw.png)

* * *

## **Profile**

  

You can view your display name, registered email address and add your profile avatar.  
Please note that only the system language is editable. If changes are needed for the display name and email address, kindly contact the project administrator for assistance.  
  
Click the button highlighted to add your avatar image.

![Edit my Account Settings](/images/kb/103000035046/HKu-wyGlO8AFnM9OlhZPvTlIPyc6xXBXLg.png)

  

Select the image you wish to upload from your device.

  

![Edit my Account Settings](/images/kb/103000035046/Rju0H4LwtXtoMZGipduCxKyR0yz66_CZ6w.png)

  
This will then show in the profile section and top right corner.  
  

![Edit my Account Settings](/images/kb/103000035046/j4tLsLo8X0ybAM_8XV5IAqp9YvCfHtzJjg.png)

  

**Preferences**   
  
From preferences, we can adjust the Language, Notifications and add a reusable signature. Click into the language bar to open the dropdown.

  

![Edit my Account Settings](/images/kb/103000035046/WLGnH3hI75ZRdln607efrbNHOh3PQo6tJQ.png)

  

From here select the preferred language.

  

![Edit my Account Settings](/images/kb/103000035046/NjO3S34cukM6z8iCwPe_GaVNRkpjctECNg.png)

  
To turn off notifications, click "**TURN OFF NOTIFICATIONS"**.

## **Credentials**

In this section, you have the option to add or modify your OpenID identity providers associated with your account, click the "+" highlighted below to add your key.

  

![Edit my Account Settings](/images/kb/103000035046/DmK1pn3-s_bDkCVPoAmw_dCcUlJICEJAxg.png)

You can then add either your API Key or OAuth application here, select either "Add API Key" or "Add OAuth application ".

1.  API
    -   [How to get my API key?](https://docs.rdrive.io/faq/setup/how-to-get-my-api-key) 
2.  OAuth application
    -   You may add or change your account openID identity providers here.

## **FAQs & Troubleshooting**

**Q: Who can edit account details within RDrive?**  
**A:** All users can update their own account details, such as name, contact information, and password. Some profile settings may require administrator permissions depending on your organisation setup.

**Q: Why can’t I save my updated account details?**  
**A:** Ensure all required fields have been completed correctly and that your internet connection is stable. If the issue continues, refresh the page and try again.

**Q: I updated my account details, but the changes are not showing—what should I do?**  
**A:** Log out of RDrive and log back in to refresh your profile data. If the changes still do not appear, clear your browser cache or contact support.
