# **Overview**

RDrive provides a range of digital solutions designed to help organisations manage quality, construction, design and health and safety processes more effectively.

  

Each solution brings the relevant forms, records, evidence and workflows into one connected environment. This helps teams replace disconnected spreadsheets, emails and paper-based processes with structured information that can be captured, reviewed and managed throughout the project.

  

RDrive solutions can support teams working both on site and in the office, helping everyone access the information they need and maintain a clear, auditable project record.

# **What can RDrive solutions help you achieve?**

Depending on your organisation’s requirements and project configuration, RDrive can help you:

-   Standardise processes across projects and teams
-   Capture information directly from site
-   Assign actions and responsibilities
-   Record photographs, documents and supporting evidence
-   Track records through review, approval and close-out
-   Connect information to project locations and drawings
-   Maintain a clear history of activity and changes
-   Monitor outstanding actions and recurring issues
-   Improve visibility through dashboards and reporting
-   Build a reliable digital record for project handover and future reference

# **Explore our solutions**

## **Quality and Construction**

Quality and Construction solutions support the processes used to inspect, verify and manage work throughout the delivery of a project.

  

They help project teams confirm that work has been completed correctly, identify where action is required and maintain the supporting evidence needed to demonstrate quality and compliance.

Information can be connected to the relevant project locations, drawings, assets and responsible teams. Records can then be followed from initial identification through inspection, review, corrective action and final close-out.

  

These solutions are commonly used by quality managers, construction managers, site teams, inspectors, subcontractors, commissioning teams and project leadership.

  

**Quality and Construction solutions can include:**

-   Snagging and de-snagging
-   Inspection requests
-   Non-conformance reports
-   Testing and commissioning
-   Asset tagging
-   Dilapidation and condition surveys
-   Site diaries
-   Variation management
-   Portfolio-wide issue intelligence

Together, these processes provide better visibility of project quality, outstanding work and recurring issues while building a structured record for handover.

## **Design**

Design solutions support the coordination and management of design-related information throughout a project.

  

They provide a structured way for project teams to raise questions, request clarification and record decisions when drawings, specifications or other design information require further detail.

  

Requests can be connected to the relevant drawings, locations, documents and supporting evidence, helping design and delivery teams understand the context of each query. Responsibilities and response dates can also be recorded, providing visibility of outstanding questions and potential delays.

  

These solutions are commonly used by design managers, architects, consultants, engineers, contractors, subcontractors and project managers.

  

**Design solutions can support:**

-   Requests for Information
-   Design queries and clarifications
-   Supporting drawings and documentation
-   Assignment to the appropriate person or organisation
-   Review and response workflows
-   Tracking of outstanding and overdue queries
-   A clear history of questions, responses and decisions

This helps reduce reliance on fragmented email conversations and creates a more complete record of how design questions and decisions were managed.

## **Health, Safety and Environment**

Health, Safety and Environment solutions help organisations manage the processes used to protect people, maintain safe working conditions and demonstrate compliance across their projects.

  

They provide structured workflows for planning safe activities, recording inspections and assessments, controlling higher-risk work and responding appropriately when an incident or concern is identified.

  

Information can be captured directly from site and supported with photographs, documents, signatures and other evidence. Actions can be assigned to the appropriate people and tracked through to completion, helping teams maintain visibility of outstanding safety requirements.

  

These solutions are commonly used by HSE managers, site managers, supervisors, operatives, contractors, permit issuers and project leadership.

  

**HSE solutions can include:**

-   Permit management
-   Safety incident reporting
-   COSHH management
-   Worker welfare
-   Training records
-   Equipment and plant safety
-   Risk assessments
-   Fire safety
-   HSE inspections and audits

Together, these processes help organisations apply more consistent safety controls, respond to concerns promptly and maintain an auditable record of HSE activity.
