**Yeah! You finish filling in relevant part(s) on a form. What's next?**

It is time to know how to change the form status so that the next party can continue working on it!  
  
How to update the status of a form on RDrive app? Let's take a look!  
  
On the form viewer, click on the status box at the top right corner

![How to update the status of a form?](/images/kb/103000035204/VXSZFQczTgG_QxwVnz_KEx2Q-V4n3dWo7A.jpeg)

Select the status, enter a comment if need, and hit "Save".

![How to update the status of a form?](/images/kb/103000035204/CD1Dvysrc_ke_wb-oT01eWSvP2izvt3ZSg.jpeg)

It's done!

<Note>
⚠️ After completing your onsite work, don't forget to synchronize. This action ensures data backup and updates for your project team.
</Note>
