A controller is a team that oversees this item. Control is first given to the team that creates the item, and can be passed along to different teams when necessary.

* * *

![Change the controller of the process](/images/kb/103000035059/R3IDBPtM_D0XYwViZNN0PfDzgPw4irUSew.jpeg)

Click the **⋮** on the top right corner of the detail page and select **Control**.

![Change the controller of the process](/images/kb/103000035059/f-qFonXpMNyHl-vAZ8sLNrJ-zGmzo5auRA.jpeg)

![Change the controller of the process](/images/kb/103000035059/cDoI-etM87oBvEvfm-qBI0mCIFLytHkAkA.jpeg)

The current controller will be shown.

By default, the created by team will be the first controller.

Click **CHANGE** to change the controller.

![Change the controller of the process](/images/kb/103000035059/pEvPFwKwy746g4BAp-x8HW97iGI_EX2PKA.jpeg)

Select team.  
**SAVE** to confirm.

![Change the controller of the process](/images/kb/103000035059/EqJ3iJFMKfDswC4kKfjAI8Qjf2vZ_diWMA.jpeg)

Updated.

![Change the controller of the process](/images/kb/103000035059/0KO9DjAcGAQ3eWsKZX2973wiH0xCkycAoA.jpeg)
