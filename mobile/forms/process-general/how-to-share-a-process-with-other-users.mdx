Sharing gives **ad-hoc read access** to other users. You can choose individual users to share with.  
  
Let's say if there are users who have edit right only. If you share a form with them, these users who have edit rights but not read access will be able to edit after you have shared with them. Those who are shared are called “participants”.  
  
To put it simple, you can share a form with other users to give them permission to see the form(s).

You will need "Share" permission to perform the "Share" action.

Here are the steps showing how to share a form to other users using RDrive app:

Go to Finder/Plan viewer, and search for a process

![How to share a process with other users?](/images/kb/103000035015/Nu_OueheqYoD4JoMEwl1WJGprx7G-s_doQ.jpeg)

![How to share a process with other users?](/images/kb/103000035015/8wuVVqPPzfiHKZVFZxVdPFBJmgUao_-24A.jpeg)

Select a process and access the form list page.

![How to share a process with other users?](/images/kb/103000035015/-NPA45tz6wFp1lZpL39PI-JxP-9ZBvPGRw.jpeg)

Click the **⋮** on the top right corner of the detail page and select **Share**.

![How to share a process with other users?](/images/kb/103000035015/Az0wl6BPccKRdxj3mFD9t7a_LHersWOkXg.jpeg)

![How to share a process with other users?](/images/kb/103000035015/IgqgoPF4iwydlAS1bwa2ymp7X5jX2iPhJw.jpeg)

The participants will be listed on the page.

Click the **+** user button to add per team or per user as a participant.

**SAVE** to confirm.

![How to share a process with other users?](/images/kb/103000035015/hqKnej6vFdWR7Mb-NLYwrqieg2jyp_-cbw.jpeg)

![How to share a process with other users?](/images/kb/103000035015/0YJDIMCnQ1WTJWD-dfQp1oUR9fNMZPtckg.jpeg)

To remove, please **swipe left** to clear.

![How to share a process with other users?](/images/kb/103000035015/iRBFWmggxqPNZEZq8jt7xMlgGBpsN41eUA.jpeg)
