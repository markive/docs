If you need to manage processes, for sure you have to know where they are.  
Let's find it out!?  
  
This article will show you how to do it on the RDrive app.

-   [via Finder](#Via-Finder)

# Via Plan Viewer

If you would like to search/find the process by drawing, this is the way for you!  
  
1\. Go to plan viewer

![How to search/find processes?](/images/kb/103000035202/fdk3Zq9h2dSEmJRazx_UOdIfSZpBXk7x2Q.jpeg)2\. Click on the drawing title at the top

![How to search/find processes?](/images/kb/103000035202/QBRjJLqrz9cwrfrQTP1sCvTClzURA-3cOA.jpeg)

You are now on the drawing list. Pick one of the drawings.![How to search/find processes?](/images/kb/103000035202/4GIQOKyLTd_nJ1un5jFQbo0-hLMB8gXUQw.jpeg)

  
Then, you can view the selected drawing as well as the location pins and the processes created.

  
Click the Memo icon above each location pin.

![How to search/find processes?](/images/kb/103000035202/szuMU7A6nW6spbCAVqMvlYr2mwqWXiDtLg.jpeg)

<Info>
What are those numbers shown on the memo? 1. The number on the memo represents the number of processes of that particular location. 2. On the app, the number includes closed processes while it does not include closed processes on the website. 3. Different users may see different numbers subject to their access permission to different process templates.
</Info>

  

Next, you will see a list of categories at that particular location! 

Kindly select the process from the **categories** > **subcategory**.

![How to search/find processes?](/images/kb/103000035202/c1-kTkD8HStEAXw5Z3OlXYSXcXusDTLGXQ.jpeg)

![How to search/find processes?](/images/kb/103000035202/anDFhThaCvQ1Vwxtt61zFvYrBniOvtc38A.jpeg)

![How to search/find processes?](/images/kb/103000035202/l_mlHN6ekIwX5coFYsLGLO0esu2qYgQMYw.jpeg)

![How to search/find processes?](/images/kb/103000035202/mrcGbTHV_JEBGQ9NA0PXtprBf8gSjE2v4g.jpeg)

<Note>
Having too many processes on the list? Alternatively, you can search the process via Finder. This would help! Let's have a look!
</Note>

## Filter

You can add **filters** by any critical to search the processes on Finder.

Click the magnifier icon at the top right corner to search for specifics.

![How to search/find processes?](/images/kb/103000035202/EAV-FIoI_VV4Q42TSn6Di6fmEj816aoqRA.jpeg)

Next, apply your search criteria to filter results.

![How to search/find processes?](/images/kb/103000035202/OgCaz7CU4J_zfu4Y_714Q0XUDEsXwR37zA.jpeg)

![How to search/find processes?](/images/kb/103000035202/ZtzczRYwB_FuRTKkFHhPHHWCbE2DmdcQ6w.jpeg)

Click on the magnifier icon to apply the search.

![How to search/find processes?](/images/kb/103000035202/Mrg4U0VL6G8m_8_3GZcGGhGpfjhOR_g3vQ.jpeg)

![How to search/find processes?](/images/kb/103000035202/acVzMQNAr7PGkxPR1sOGmVbjgu6CqMVOrQ.jpeg)

You may save the filter selection as a **[bookmark](https://docs.rdrive.io/mobile/general/save-a-bookmark-for-filter)** for next time.

  

* * *

<Note>
Don't panic if you cannot see all of the processes created. Users may have different permission settings associated with them. Therefore, it is possible that you are only viewing some of the processes that you are allowed to.
</Note>
