When you get mentioned in a comment, you should receive an email notification.

As soon as you reply to it, this triggers an email notification back after sync.

Here is how you can reply to a comment via APP:

Go to Finder and access the process and form.

![How to reply to a comment on a form?](/images/kb/103000035076/Qr_4TdTDTtB3ABJxbhe12GHJzRaJK_isEA.jpeg)

![How to reply to a comment on a form?](/images/kb/103000035076/3_BVMyw8VgsM8bGZF5NI-2IRt9-ZIE5dSQ.jpeg)

![How to reply to a comment on a form?](/images/kb/103000035076/0_RxDlK0n-yAxnWNc4JkPbPGupQJbWt10Q.jpeg)

![How to reply to a comment on a form?](/images/kb/103000035076/eYJhYweWkcwxLh_vU4U5ReNSiPTHkyUEQA.jpeg)

A form is now opened in the form viewer, click ⋮ to select **Comment**

![How to reply to a comment on a form?](/images/kb/103000035076/ikzCYFCzbwh_bXq99gSuJIlj43BJx5upMA.jpeg)

![How to reply to a comment on a form?](/images/kb/103000035076/RLaU_eraabKj1elzvt3JZp5jWJRPiLcA4w.jpeg)

You may see a comment here and be allowed to reply.

Click the **REPLY** button to add a reply

![How to reply to a comment on a form?](/images/kb/103000035076/8JeOkNklYf7xomB6S5azAajMmdg28dtCcw.jpeg)

You can either  
1️⃣ Simply leave a reply to the comment creator.  
2️⃣ Leave a reply and @mention user by clicking notify user icon

If you go for 2️⃣ , once user(s) is picked , click **OK**

![How to reply to a comment on a form?](/images/kb/103000035076/drPs6WlBEciAP6ZfuBxMElQXbA7RsJBGKA.jpeg)

When you finish entering your reply, click **REPLY** to save.

![How to reply to a comment on a form?](/images/kb/103000035076/21n6Zy0xTHDhFY0eO2oEeSSzXNPK2mZx5g.jpeg)

![How to reply to a comment on a form?](/images/kb/103000035076/cnaAsdM3vXMSibYv5J1m_k-1q9AbFsRu7g.jpeg)

After it is synced, the user(s) you picked will receive an email / app notification!  
You've got it!

  
_Before syncing, you may edit and delete the reply.  
Once the data is synced, you will not be able to edit and delete it._

  

  

<Note>
⚠️ After completing your onsite work, don't forget to synchronize. This backs up your data and updates your project team.
</Note>
