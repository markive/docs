It is very common that different supporting documents, related issues/records and processes come along with the form itself.

Here's how you can do to view it.

* * *

  

Go to Finder and access the **Process** > **Form**.

![Where to view the linked item(s) of the form?](/images/kb/103000035078/GIvmPR9MDZbeasirpUlT5N0psz4dnCHl1Q.jpeg)

A form is now opened in the form viewer, click ⋮ to select "**Links**".

![Where to view the linked item(s) of the form?](/images/kb/103000035078/UUO05injrAck-nU55yYNXAeo7Irc6rhdbg.jpeg)

![Where to view the linked item(s) of the form?](/images/kb/103000035078/br-XIYsd7kUBOIJqzzCKa3dW8lPmGUBW3w.jpeg)

The linked item would display here by field. General click into it and view the linked item.
