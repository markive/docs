## **Overview**  
  

-   **Purpose:** This guide introduces the RDrive app's in-app notification features, explaining how notifications are delivered and managed within the app.
-   **Who It’s For:** RDrive app users  
      
    

<Note>
Prerequisites: 
- RDrive app installed on the device.
- User has approved the device permissions for RDrive app to push notifications.
</Note>

* * *

## **In-App Notification Features**  
  

✅ **Comment Notifications**  
Users will receive in-app notifications when new comments are added to items they are involved with, such as mentions or replies.  

✅ **Status Change Notifications (Subscription-Based)**

Users who subscribe to specific items will be notified whenever the status of those items changes.

✅ **Un-Sync Reminder**

If the app remains idle for more than 15 minutes with unsynced data, a reminder notification will be pushed to alert the user to sync their data.

**_Note:_**_  
Notifications will only appear if the user has granted the app permission to send notifications on their device. Ensure notifications are enabled in the device settings to receive alerts._

* * *

# **FAQs & Troubleshooting**  
  

**Q: I am not receiving notifications. What should I do?**  
A: Confirm that your device allows RDrive to push notifications. Check app settings and device notification settings.

**Q: Can I customize which notifications I receive?**  
A: Yes, you can subscribe to specific items to receive only status change notifications for those.
