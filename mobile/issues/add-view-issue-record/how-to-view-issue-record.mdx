When dealing with the issues or records, it is crucial to know their location. 

There are two methods to locate and manage them: using the  [Finder](#h_7ef0096ec4) or [on the drawing.](#h_bacfc02574)

* * *

## Via **Finder**

Click the menu icon ≡ on the top left corner to unfold the Menu, and select **the Subcategory finder**.

![How to view issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035181/JjZvukV2ComZVbCLNo90vOHOYBCcOtfztA.jpeg)

![How to view issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035181/3fWj6yvvA4Q9-n-XQdCzHF1iagpM7t0MIQ.jpeg)

All the issue / record that belonged to this subcategory will list out here.  
You may click the magnifier icon at the top right corner to search for specific.

![How to view issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035181/vSJVw6_KLTkAW0BJSnZ_HD9ZA58rRIXd7Q.jpeg)

Next, apply your search criteria to filter results.

![How to view issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035181/i0NDbQEyxU3s3G7SbQPRV_r3Jsiq6dSkuQ.jpeg)

![How to view issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035181/YvVvBeUGDILFYoGiTTMb3GJYYNd5vy0uCw.jpeg)

Click at the magnifier icon to apply search.

![How to view issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035181/MIN_EYcB8VZvoXmN71gTI9to6Kv0qW8OUg.jpeg)

![How to view issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035181/ymSvO_IV9j3ZxBmMoqstXJrIDs0ExAW7qQ.jpeg)

<Note>
And you may save the filter selection as a bookmark for the next time.
</Note>

* * *

## Via **Plan viewer**

Alternatively, you can return to the Plan Viewer to manage issues or records by location.

  

Select **drawing** at the top, click on any pins and view full details of the record.
