The associated item will be shown on the detail page. Simply click on it to view the linked item(s).

It's typical for various supporting documents, related issues/records, and processes to accompany the form.

To view these linked items, follow these steps:

    1. Open the issue/record details from the Plan Viewer or Finder.

![How to view the linked item(s) of the issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035077/TJ_d59jc07b3ethgcySUuZjwadNLVP_Gog.jpeg)

    2. Click on the ⋮ in the upper right corner of the detail page and choose "Links."

![How to view the linked item(s) of the issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035077/KZOJUu7lUIBMlAJDDKFvhqG9ZVZwUKoHFQ.jpeg)

![How to view the linked item(s) of the issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035077/abuvRzXnh-LVrdiqMKx3a5T0FlpACTsVIw.jpeg)

The linked item will be displayed based on the field(s). 

Click on it to view.
