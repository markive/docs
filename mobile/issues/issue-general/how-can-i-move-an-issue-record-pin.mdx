title: "How can I move an issue/record pin?"
description: "⚠️ Pin is only able to move with before sync."
---

⚠️ Pin is only able to move with before sync.

If you need to move a pin to another spot, here is what you can do:

  
**Long press** on an issue from the plan viewer to open a menu.  
Select **Move.**

![How can I move an issue/record pin?](/images/kb/103000035169/iu56mRPk8E-o3JTHeikW2Wr0UyxwdW0riA.jpeg)

![How can I move an issue/record pin?](/images/kb/103000035169/XJ4ysAU6NousD_XOc8wZP1K8r-2ZwBokHA.jpeg)

Drag the issue to change its position.  
**PLACE** to save.
