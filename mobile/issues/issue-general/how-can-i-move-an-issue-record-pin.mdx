### **Overview**

_How to move a pin on the app in RDrive._

-   **Purpose:** How to move a pin using the app.
-   **Who It’s For:** General users 

<Note>
Prerequisites: Have raised the item and not yet synced the device
</Note>

  

  

  

  

  
**Please note: You can only move a Pin before you  sync your device.**  

If you need to move a pin to another spot, here is what you can do:

  
**Hold and press** on an issue pin from the plan viewer to open a menu.  

![How can I move an issue/record pin?](/images/kb/103000035169/8Ywmrb4t2lbmwvh-fyKOlJT25y2C0nDfTQ.png)

  

This will then bring up the menu for this item, select move.  

  

![How can I move an issue/record pin?](/images/kb/103000035169/zhPFY0ZlCaGx5BcNom76Pz9gBAscmwyW0g.png)

Drag the Pin to change its position as required, then select **PLACE** to save.

### **FAQs & Troubleshooting**

**Q: Can I move an issue or record pin after it has been created?**  
**A:** Yes. If you have the appropriate permissions, you can reposition an issue or record pin to a new location on the drawing, plan, or map.

**Q: Why would I need to move a pin?**  
**A:** Pins may need to be moved if they were placed in the wrong location, if the issue relates to a different area than originally recorded, or if the drawing has been updated.

**Q: Why can't I move a pin?**  
**A:** You may not have the required permissions, or the item may be in a status that prevents location changes. Contact your project administrator if you require additional access.
