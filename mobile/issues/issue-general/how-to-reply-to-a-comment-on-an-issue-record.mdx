title: "How to reply to a comment on an issue/record?"
description: "When you get mentioned in a comment, you should receive an email notification."
---

When you get mentioned in a comment, you should receive an email notification.

As soon as you reply to it, this triggers an email notification back.

Here is what you can reply to a comment:

Open the issue/record detail from the Plan viewer or Finder.

![How to reply to a comment on an issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035177/FLpcdTjCYjG7I0x7DowI6vSmo7QhX-2j2g.jpeg)

Click **Comment**.

![How to reply to a comment on an issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035177/0I7J9sJn3G-v0FI2kD_jrz5airkNVBqeYQ.jpeg)

Click **Reply** icon of the comment.

![How to reply to a comment on an issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035177/6bJhqjBjnWnhmyZ3i3GWiygH3sRMEpS6-g.jpeg)

Write down your message and click **Reply** to save.

![How to reply to a comment on an issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035177/e9aasQGHcwCGZylGuAWcZeJb8eAGYO3UKA.jpeg)

You've got it!

  
Before sync, it is possible you edit and delete the comment.  
Once the data is synced, you will not be able to edit and delete the comment.

![How to reply to a comment on an issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035177/Q3o9CK_Ogfuuz85U7mCTi2Ep1EcngwjuWQ.jpeg)

<Note>
⚠️ After completing your onsite work, don't forget to synchronize. This action ensures data backup and updates for your project team.
</Note>
