A controller is a team that oversees this item. Control is first given to the team that creates the item, and can be passed along to different teams when necessary.

* * *

![How to change the controller of the issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035062/Fk9JKOAYFNET5F3A_QnZoohAVSCqefQjqQ.jpeg)

Click the **⋮** on the top right corner of the detail page and select **Control**.

![How to change the controller of the issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035062/1ZYJbmzMQgfXDu8R74SSbwBokZCw7weALQ.jpeg)

![How to change the controller of the issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035062/TZMCouIxOFoeVxUK8CftGbCobpl3RLobDA.jpeg)

The current controller will be shown.

By default, the created by team will be the first controller.

Click **CHANGE** to change the controller.

![How to change the controller of the issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035062/jg9ZhgaNxALsroQDoUJYe6C3PMOLhxf3UA.jpeg)

Select team.  
**SAVE** to confirm.

![How to change the controller of the issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035062/3df5pK3l70EUBcxCukHr4TvAzTD29YYNYg.jpeg)

Updated.

![How to change the controller of the issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035062/8PYYKuqlaYe_NhHvvhYxonRuzTTbOrvigw.jpeg)
