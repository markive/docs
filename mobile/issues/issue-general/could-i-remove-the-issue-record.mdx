---
title: "Could I remove the issue/record?"
description: "Before you synchronise, you can delete issue/record you created."
---

**Before you synchronise**, you can delete issue/record you created.

**After you synchronise**, delete right is regarding how is the permission set up by the project admin.  
Normally, only project administrators can delete an issue/record.

**Long press** on an issue from the plan viewer to open a menu.  
Select **Delete.**

![Could I remove the issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035167/oa-l-wt62lFqz0is-D566nIbkiuELfbVFg.jpeg)

![Could I remove the issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035167/KGVNuYyD3xiu9nhXshwk-AOyx9xnNVJz3Q.jpeg)

**OK** to confirm.

![Could I remove the issue/record?](/images/kb/103000035167/MEHL0UM-B4AwYh3otlBuG0fP26omMPQhkw.jpeg)

<Info>
⚠️ Beware!: once you have deleted the item, you will not be able to undo it.
</Info>
