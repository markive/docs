---

You may check on the subscriber list to know who is watching this issue/record and add a watcher to **receive the notification** of this issue/record.

When subscribing, a user will simultaneously become a participant, so he/she will always be able to read whatever he/she is subscribing to.

* * *

Open the issue/record detail from the Plan viewer or Finder.

Click the **⋮** on the top right corner of the detail page and select **Subscribe**.

The subscriber will be list on the page if have.

Click the **+** user button and enter the user name search to add as a subscriber.

**SAVE** to confirm.
