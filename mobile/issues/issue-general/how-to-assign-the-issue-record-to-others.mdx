An issue can be initially assigned to a team and further delegated to specific team members who are currently responsible for the issue/record. All team members have permission to view the issue/record, and specific members will be notified of updates.

To change the current assignee, you must have the **ASSIGN** permission. Navigate to the issue assign dialogue to specify the current assignee. You can find the initial assignee in the issue itself and the current assignee in the **ASSIGN** dialogue.

Once the assignee is changed, the new assignee will be notified. The current assignee is the individual currently handling the issue.

To change the assignee:

![How to assign the issue/record to others?](/images/kb/103000035174/M21Sdmml-cQY0QR3SXWvWL330ql0nttvng.jpeg)

1\. Click the ⋮ on the top right corner of the detail page and select **Assign**.

![How to assign the issue/record to others?](/images/kb/103000035174/m-DPfRRmmgZXIHGmwYgs9NXNVR_m_vh13A.jpeg)

![How to assign the issue/record to others?](/images/kb/103000035174/Pscl79ktwl9gdGKnHHnHXtNh19_wZQYMXg.jpeg)

2\. If a current assignee is present, it will be displayed.

3\. Click **CHANGE** to modify the assignee.

![How to assign the issue/record to others?](/images/kb/103000035174/da68ExZQvqMw5TViyRgTnVOP9Ra8VVMdaA.jpeg)

4\. To assign to others or change the current assignee, select the team and add the user(s). 

    Click **SAVE** to confirm.

![How to assign the issue/record to others?](/images/kb/103000035174/474JafAx4GocbGqSvNZBNYOpped61b9sDA.jpeg)

![How to assign the issue/record to others?](/images/kb/103000035174/8TjpJDdDFl8CvEi9qjG1cqZ-HhRTG-DZKw.jpeg)

The assignee is now updated. 

![How to assign the issue/record to others?](/images/kb/103000035174/2HTJo8loAbueiLIwGS-jJSTTK1srXa7Lsg.jpeg)

To remove a user as an assignee, **swipe left** to delete.

![How to assign the issue/record to others?](/images/kb/103000035174/PcHmXDEXn03GEVbx3nILk8WhRdsmNbHp9g.jpeg)
