### **FAQs & Troubleshooting**

**Q: Can I update an issue after it has been created?**  
**A:** Yes. Open the issue within the app and edit the relevant fields, such as status, description, priority, due date, or assigned user, depending on your permissions.

**Q: Why can’t I edit an issue?**  
**A:** You may not have the required permissions, or the issue may be in a status that restricts further changes. Contact your project administrator if you believe you should have access.

**Q: Can I update an issue while working offline?**  
**A:** Yes. Changes made offline will be stored on your device and uploaded the next time you sync the app with an internet connection.
