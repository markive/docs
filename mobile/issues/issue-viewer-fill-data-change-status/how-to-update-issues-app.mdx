Once an issue is created, various parties may participate in the follow-up process, involving tasks like [editing](#%e2%80%8bto-edit--) and [updating the status](#%e2%80%8bto-change-status--). The platform is optimally utilized for transparency and efficiency when different parties engage in the entire issue rectification process.

Editing an issue encompasses tasks such as:

1.  Re-assigning the issue to another team
2.  Changing issue descriptions
3.  Updating due dates, and more

* * *

## To **Edit** -

1\. Open the issue/record details from the Plan Viewer or Finder.

![How to update issues? (APP)](/images/kb/103000035180/nGpPpTn24zs2NGVT0OXOHEYL8V1MK8e3XQ.jpeg)

    2. Click on the Edit icon to make changes.

![How to update issues? (APP)](/images/kb/103000035180/idM-HypEBwGbAIIp_shGF5cRq6DE4VqA7Q.jpeg)

    3. Scroll down and click Save to confirm the modifications.

![How to update issues? (APP)](/images/kb/103000035180/4Dg9G_bfVK3q5923lEW3JeiZIYgfpn7kqA.jpeg)

## To **Change Status** -

1\. Open the issue/record details from the Plan Viewer or Finder.

![How to update issues? (APP)](/images/kb/103000035180/OJeen4UNqm50yLcw8U4DJfVoGdY6q0xeWA.jpeg)

2\. Click on the current status label to initiate the status change.

![How to update issues? (APP)](/images/kb/103000035180/pzsihwmTAopBFiAT-uZg0KHwWvYCQkdysQ.jpeg)

3\. Update the status, add a note if necessary, and click **Save** to complete the process.

![How to update issues? (APP)](/images/kb/103000035180/7jaCbiq9Jai4du3szlbDkDKeP2vIG4L11Q.jpeg)

<Note>
⚠️ After completing your onsite work, don't forget to synchronize. This action ensures data backup and updates for your project team.
</Note>
