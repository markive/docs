**To add a photo in a photo field,** **you may add from the photo gallery or taking photos on-site by tapping the photo field.**

## **Taking photos on-site by tapping the photo field.**  

Click the **camera icon** to open the camera. 

![How can I add photo to the issue/record? (APP)](/images/kb/103000035172/0Siw2C76Zg2a_1vfLUU261NRtw-PfpNRjQ.jpeg)

## **Select from the photo gallery**

Click the **photo icon** to open the photo library to select a photo.

![How can I add photo to the issue/record? (APP)](/images/kb/103000035172/wBIdCRj_Zl0NYfIyUq7QyIvm1zcx-JuL4A.jpeg)
