### **Overview**

_How to add photos from the camera and gallery using the app in RDrive._

-   **Purpose:** How to add photos to RDrive using the app.
-   **Who It’s For:** General users

<Note>
Prerequisites: Able to reach and edit issue
</Note>

  

To add a photo in a photo field, you may add from the photo gallery or taking photos on-site by tapping the photo field.

  

## Taking photos on-site by tapping the photo field.  

Click the **camera icon** to open the camera and take and add a photo directly from the RDrive app.

![How can I add photo to the issue/record? (APP)](/images/kb/103000035172/ma-K8O9sb3ErVWqzO7WgD5E6XCCuFgS88g.png)

## **Select from the photo gallery**

Click the **Gallery icon** to open the photo library to select a photo from your device storage.

![How can I add photo to the issue/record? (APP)](/images/kb/103000035172/NSY-wpVIz2UZ2uNKTD1rFSECDXjGmb1u9g.png)

  

### **FAQs & Troubleshooting**

**Q: Can I take a photo directly from the app?**  
**A:** Yes. When adding a photo, you can choose to take a new photo using your device's camera or select an existing image from your photo gallery.

**Q: Why can't I add a photo to an issue or record?**  
**A:** Ensure the app has permission to access your device's camera and photo library. You should also verify that you have the required permissions to edit the issue or record.

**Q: What image formats are supported?**  
**A:** Most standard image formats, such as JPG and PNG, are supported. The exact formats available may depend on your device and app version.
