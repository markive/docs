### **Overview**

_You may wish to reduce the image quality over app speed, this guide covers how to control image quality and camera function, should your device experience crashes in the plan viewer, try reducing the image quality._

-   **Purpose:** How and where within the app to change the in-app camera functionality and control image quality.
-   **Who It’s For:** All general app users

<Note>
Prerequisites: RDrive account, App Installed
</Note>

## **Step-by-Step Instructions**

### **✅ Step 1: Accessing the shortcuts and tools panel**

1.  Once logged into your app and project, from the main menu pull the shortcut bar up from the bottom.
2.  Select the "Settings" option.![In-App Camera Function & Reducing Image Quality](/images/kb/103000361615/4vvWp536-6kpOqfmVU9zmYWFO1gGPq67Pw.png)

### **✅ Step 2: Toggle on the in app camera service**

1.  Scroll to the bottom of the "Settings" page to the "Camera" section.
2.  Toggle on the "Use in-app camera service".
3.  Hit "Save" in the top right corner.![In-App Camera Function & Reducing Image Quality](/images/kb/103000361615/6gSgXbF5nrH4yW1sCyRdA_8U8xji2Bh1Mw.png)

### **✅ Step 3: Changing the drawing image quality**

1.  From the main menu, select the "View Plans" tab.
2.  Click the 3 dots in the top right and select "Display Settings"  
    ![In-App Camera Function & Reducing Image Quality](/images/kb/103000361615/V_Dwyk3oXkIOAPNjOsUhYT_-Aar3CwEQyg.jpeg) 
3.  Toggle the "Drawing image quality" option on and select "Low" to improve app performance.
4.  Select "Save"![In-App Camera Function & Reducing Image Quality](/images/kb/103000361615/Vhvkbl7Dt0GXdBeoQogfNzSjNcC2jPwwIQ.png)

## **FAQs & Troubleshooting**

**Q: Can I improve app speed by changing image quality?**  
A: Yes. Reducing drawing image quality can improve performance, especially on devices experiencing crashes or slowdowns in the plan viewer.

**Q: How do I change the drawing image quality?**  
A: In the app, open a project and go to **View Plans → ⋯ (three dots) → Display Settings**. Toggle **Drawing image quality** on, select **Low**, and tap **Save** to apply the change.

**Q: Can I use the in-app camera instead of my device’s default camera?**  
A: Yes. Open the shortcut bar from the bottom of the screen, select **Settings**, scroll to the **Camera** section, toggle **Use in-app camera service** on, and tap **Save**.
