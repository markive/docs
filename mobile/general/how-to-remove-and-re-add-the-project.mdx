### **Overview**

_How to remove a project from the mobile app and re-add that back to the device_

-   **Purpose:** To remove and re-add a project for data clarity
-   **Who It’s For:** Users experiencing syncing issues or data not matching up

<Note>
Prerequisites: Working from the mobile app, Internet connection
</Note>

<Info>
Please note, deleting a project from your app in this way removes this from your device only and does not delete the project for others. Always ensure you sync your device before removing the project as any un-synced data will be lost when the project is deleted.
</Info>

  
**Step-by-Step Instructions**

### **✅ Step 1: Navigating to the projects page**

1.  When working in the mobile app, from the main menu, press the back arrow to go to the project management screen.![How to Remove and Re-add the Project?](/images/kb/103000359864/rqAJDghFFuWNYUoe7ExUEAlhv0wxrOHQUw.png)
2.  From here select "Projects" to see all active projects you currently have access too.

### **✅ Step 2: Removing the Project from your App**

1.  From the Projects screen you should see a list of all projects you have access too, dragging the project name to the left will allow you to delete a project from your app![How to Remove and Re-add the Project?](/images/kb/103000359864/oaye1PAEp03l4MkFRs2rRnxA0XZAn25-AA.jpg)
2.  Drag the project you wish to remove and select "Delete" and confirm by selecting "Ok"![How to Remove and Re-add the Project?](/images/kb/103000359864/Sm4lvZlg0DeChXu2eOOCZpQQu5fW-B9jhg.jpg)

### **✅ Step 3: Adding the project back to our device**

1.  Now we have removed the project from our device we can re-add this, Click the "+" in the top left corner to see all available projects for download![How to Remove and Re-add the Project?](/images/kb/103000359864/G16VpKKvgibo8I6lsZ3iSrOQG9Vl1KkQew.jpg)
2.  Click the download button on all required projects to then re-download this to your device for use![How to Remove and Re-add the Project?](/images/kb/103000359864/ZSQqkFiv3sZO4ve3RF7NCzn-Nf07OcBiNg.jpg)

You can now click into and work on the project downloaded.

## **FAQs & Troubleshooting**

**Q: Will deleting a project from my app delete it for other users?**  
**A:** No, deleting a project from your mobile app only removes it from your device. Other users will still have access to the project.

**Q: What happens if I don’t sync before deleting the project?**  
**A:** Any changes or data not synced before deletion will be permanently lost. Always sync your app before removing a project.

**Q: I don’t see my project in the list after trying to re-add it—what should I do?**  
**A:** Make sure you're connected to the internet and have the correct permissions for the project. If it still doesn't appear, log out and back into the app, then try again.

**Q: The app is not syncing even after re-adding the project. What should I check?**  
**A:** Ensure you have a stable internet connection and that you're logged in correctly. If the issue continues, restart the app or contact support for further assistance.

**Q: I accidentally deleted the wrong project—can I get it back?**  
**A:** Yes, simply re-add the project by clicking the "+" icon and downloading it again. Just make sure you had synced any previous changes before deletion.
