### **Overview**

_A brief introduction explaining the purpose of this guide._

-   **Purpose:** To ensure your RDrive mobile app stays up-to date with the server and avoids data loss
-   **Who It’s For:** All mobile app users working on-site or offline

<Note>
Prerequisites: Installed RDrive app and an active project, internet connection
</Note>

  

  

  

  

* * *

## **Step-by-Step Instructions**

### **✅ Step 1:** Understanding Synchronisation

What is Syncing?  
  
Synchronisation connects your mobile device with the RDrive server via the internet. This ensures all data—both newly created and updated—is aligned across all users and platforms.

Why is it Important?

-   Keeps everyone working with the most current information  
      
    
-   Prevents data conflicts or overwrites  
      
    
-   Ensures your work is backed up and not lost

  

### **✅ Step 2:** Sync Before Going On-Site

1.  Ensure you’re connected to a stable network (Wi-Fi or mobile data).  
      
    
2.  Open the RDrive App.  
      
    
3.  From the main menu or project screen, locate and tap the “Sync” button, changes needing to be send up will mean the sync button will appear blue.  
      
    
4.  Wait until the synchronisation completes. You’ll receive a confirmation message once done.  
      
    

This step downloads the latest project data and records to your device, so you're ready to work offline.

### **✅ Step 3:** Work Offline in the Field

-   You can now create records, defects, or forms even without internet access.  
      
    
-   All your offline entries are stored locally on your device.  
      
    

Continue capturing data as normal—syncing is not required during this step.

  

### **✅ Step 4:** Sync After Completing Work

1.  Reconnect your device to the internet.  
      
    
2.  Return to the main screen or project menu in the RDrive App.  
      
    
3.  Tap the “Sync” button again.  
      
    
4.  Allow the app to upload all changes and download any updates from others.  
      
    

This ensures all your entries are backed up and accessible to other team members.

  

* * *

## Best Practices & Tips

-   Always Sync Twice a Day  
    Before going to the site and After finishing field work.  
      
    
-   Check Sync Status  
    Look for “Last Synced” timestamps or success messages to confirm completion.  
      
    
-   Avoid Data Conflicts  
    Don’t create duplicate records before syncing existing data from the server.  
      
    
-   Enable Auto-Sync (if supported)  
    Some versions of the app may allow background syncing when a network is detected.  
      
    

* * *

## FAQs & Troubleshooting

Q: What happens if I don’t sync?  
 A: Your data remains stored only on your device. If the device is lost or data is corrupted, un-synced records may be permanently lost.  
Q: Can I sync using mobile data?  
 A: Yes. However, for large syncs (photos, attachments), Wi-Fi is recommended to save bandwidth.  
Q: How will I know if sync failed?  
 A: The app will display an error message or syncing status bar. Retry when a stable connection is available.

* * *

By regularly syncing the RDrive App, you ensure smooth team collaboration, accurate project data, and robust protection against data loss.

Keep your syncs consistent—your project depends on it.
