### **FAQs & Troubleshooting**

**Q: What is a saved filter bookmark?**  
**A:** A saved filter bookmark allows you to save a set of filter criteria for quick access in the future. This helps you avoid reapplying the same filters each time you view your data.

**Q: Can I save multiple filter bookmarks?**  
**A:** Yes. You can create multiple bookmarks for different views, projects, teams, statuses, or reporting requirements.

**Q: Will my saved bookmark be visible to other users?**  
**A:** In most cases, saved bookmarks are personal to your account. If your system supports shared bookmarks, your administrator will be able to advise on availability and permissions.
