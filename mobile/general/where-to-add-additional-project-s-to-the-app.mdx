---
title: "Where to add additional project(s) to the APP?"
description: "To download additional project(s), unfold the side menu and click Projects."
---

To download additional project(s), unfold the side menu and click **Projects**.

![Where to add additional project(s) to the APP?](/images/kb/103000035074/pmPq2lRcQoZP-2SXi8s3Xw7aU36Qw6zJQg.jpeg)

![Where to add additional project(s) to the APP?](/images/kb/103000035074/t-B3T9DsDm_tP3venXnRNGzKzcVvSPel1w.jpeg)

Press the "**+**" button at the top right corner to explore the project which you may download to access.

Click on the project to start the download.

![Where to add additional project(s) to the APP?](/images/kb/103000035074/XqvXRNxNQ675Tjq_bZg-Lcm5B2Bs3xDKVQ.jpeg)
