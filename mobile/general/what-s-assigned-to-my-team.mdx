### **✅ Step 1: Accessing my Assigned Items**

1.  From the main menu screen we should see the option for **"Assigned Items"** tab with a number of items assigned to your team. Click your team name under **"Assigned Items"** to open this.![What's Assigned to my Team?](/images/kb/103000398324/24GqjmnJbG-t-7lmzyGBW0E10HNiMTr5bQ.png)
2.  This will then show the item/record types and the amounts of each at the end, clicking this will then expand these items to view.![What's Assigned to my Team?](/images/kb/103000398324/kt4qS3DIJUI3NutmeB42bb321-rZOzIYaA.png)
3.  Expand the item type required then click into this, we will select **"Defects"** in this example.![What's Assigned to my Team?](/images/kb/103000398324/HNu3qkMABpricDDKMUIGdjJ4qihXeyYhIg.png)

## **FAQs & Troubleshooting**

**Q: Why can't I seen an item another user can?**  
**A:** This is typically down to items being assigned to another team you aren't working in, if this is for your team please sync and this should update the items assigned.

**Q: Why can't I press "Save"?**  
**A:** This is usually due to a mandatory ("\*") field not being completed, fill this field in and you should be able to continue.
