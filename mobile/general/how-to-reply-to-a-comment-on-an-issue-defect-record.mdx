## **Step-by-Step Instructions**

### **✅ Step 1: Navigate to the issue/form with the comment**

1.  From the app main menu, click the "View Plans" Tab.![How to reply to a comment on an issue/defect/record?](/images/kb/103000361634/OfQWoUtfC2UC2-x7hW5j4Y-0WxTrXw8e7Q.png)
2.  Navigate through the drill downs to the location the comment appears.![How to reply to a comment on an issue/defect/record?](/images/kb/103000361634/uSOsSb4_TUVQjOfTenPUSJyXVlhRh9XYnA.png)
3.  Select the pin with the comment to open the issue information.![How to reply to a comment on an issue/defect/record?](/images/kb/103000361634/AyVs7m1xScbrskbz44NMntYyjPHEavMj5Q.png)

### **✅ Step 2: Viewing the comments tab**

1.  Once viewing the issue or record, either swipe left to bring up the "Comments" tab or select the text icon from the bottom, a blue number will show how many comments currently are on the issue/record.![How to reply to a comment on an issue/defect/record?](/images/kb/103000361634/JhXRr99hPOFQlYR4z_qmve1viohholddUA.png)
2.  Select the arrow icon to reply to the comment, add your text and select "Reply" to add the comment.![How to reply to a comment on an issue/defect/record?](/images/kb/103000361634/Xxg-TkgpknAhonuuBZ1mMDXw2ZRFYLeSVQ.png)
3.  You comment will then appear above the original comment. Please note the "Not yet synchronised" tag indicating you need to sync for other users to see these changes.![How to reply to a comment on an issue/defect/record?](/images/kb/103000361634/4YKefvHlB8U-As7zPDMquSI4C9oHyg6Flg.png)

### **✅ Step 3: Syncing your changes** 

1.  Once you have then completed any work, coming back to the main menu will then show the sync icon in blue, this indicates a change has been made offline and requires syncing, click the blue sync icon to send these changes to the server.![How to reply to a comment on an issue/defect/record?](/images/kb/103000361634/ftaUpw3dbYOBgxny0eQXagYk8gshrR9-Ew.png)

**Please note: While working offline it is best practice to sync at the beginning of the work day and at the end once the work is complete, any un-synced data will not be shown online unless synced first.

## **FAQs & Troubleshooting**

**Q: Can I reply to comments on issues or forms in RDrive?**  
**A:** Yes. You can open any issue or form with an existing comment and add your reply directly in the app.

**Q: How do I reply to a comment?**  
**A:** In the app, go to **View Plans**, navigate to the pin containing the comment, and open the issue or record. Swipe left to the **Comments** tab or tap the text icon. Select the arrow icon next to the comment, type your reply, and tap **Reply**. Your comment will appear above the original with a “Not yet synchronised” label until you sync.

**Q: How do I make sure my replies are visible to others?**  
**A:** From the main menu, tap the blue **sync** icon to upload your changes. It’s best practice to sync at the start and end of each workday when working offline so all updates are available to the team.
