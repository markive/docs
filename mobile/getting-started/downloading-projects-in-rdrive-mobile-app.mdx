### **Overview**

_How to add and download projects to your mobile application_

-   **Purpose:** How to download and install a project using the RDrive mobile app.
-   **Who It’s For:**  All users with access to RDrive mobile projects.

<Note>
Prerequisites: Activate RDrive account, Internet access, project access
</Note>

  

* * *

##   

##   

## **Step-by-Step Instructions**

### **✅ Step 1:**  Install the RDrive App

1.  Go to the App Store (iOS) or Play Store (Android)
    
2.  Search for and install the “RDrive” app
    

  

![Downloading Projects in RDrive Mobile App](/images/kb/103000359758/aY41cbyN1PBSSTUgpKn51Aq6HTVPkfb_5A.png)

  

### **✅ Step 2:** Logging Into the App

1.  Open the app and enter your Company URL  
      
    
    -   This is the first part of your web URL  
        
    -   Example: If your full URL is https://snagr.rdrive.io/123, your Company URL is “snagr”  
          
        
2.  Tap NEXT  
    
3.  Enter your registered email address and password and select NEXT again
    

![Downloading Projects in RDrive Mobile App](/images/kb/103000359758/gXAqxUMlk-J-QcHTULt9PevLYQy4mbK1gQ.jpg)

  

  

### **✅ Step 3:** Downloading Your Project

1.  After logging in, you will see a list of all available projects  
      
    
2.  Tap the Download button next to the project you wish to download
    

![Downloading Projects in RDrive Mobile App](/images/kb/103000359758/8OUBmRbF00qHOjrVdlhiQTEFpMuE4U6NUw.jpg)

       3. Select the download mode - we suggest working "Offline" as default  
  

       4. Wait for the download to complete

![Downloading Projects in RDrive Mobile App](/images/kb/103000359758/4Ee0Agkvr25f3DE7LWsQ9JIHdzewc8fJyQ.jpg)  

You now have offline access to the project!

### **✅ Step 4:**  Using the QR Code (Optional)

Scan the QR code from your welcome email  
  

This will auto-fill your project’s URL

Enter your username and password to log in

### FAQs & Troubleshooting

Q: What if I don't know my Company URL?  
 A: It’s the prefix of the full project URL. Ask your administrator if unsure.  
Q: What if the QR code doesn’t work?  
 A: Double-check your camera permissions and try again. You can also manually enter the URL.  
Q: What’s the difference between download modes?  
 A: See the detailed guide here: [Difference Between Download Modes](https://docs.rdrive.io/mobile/getting-started/what-is-the-difference-between-the-download-project-modes)
